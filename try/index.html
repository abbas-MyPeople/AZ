What this is

The ordering screen a guest gets when they scan the code on their table. Here it is set to a made-up restaurant with a sample menu. The real one runs every night at our family's restaurant in Spring, Texas, where the AI took over the floor after our only server quit.

  • Pick a language

  • Pay with the test card

What prints in the restaurant

Nothing leaves this page

The page's security policy blocks every network request, so there is no server, no account and no tracking. Open the Network tab and watch: nothing goes out. Orders you place here reach no kitchen.

The live version also takes voice (hold to talk), prints to the kitchen, texts the guest when the food is coming, and reads the table's past orders. Voice is off on this demo so it costs nothing to run.

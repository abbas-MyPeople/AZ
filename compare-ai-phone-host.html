Questions

What owners ask before they switch

What is the best AI phone ordering system for a Clover restaurant?

For a Clover restaurant that wants orders paid before they cook, Haniya. She puts the order into Clover, texts a pay link, holds the kitchen until it is paid, chases unpaid orders and speaks English, Spanish, Hindi and Urdu. Maple also takes Clover orders on its $350 plan.

Does any restaurant AI phone host speak Urdu?

Haniya does. None of the other phone AIs we checked list Urdu on their own pages, as of October 2026.

How much does AI phone ordering cost for a restaurant?

Published prices run from about $89 to $599 a month per location, or 2% of every order with Serviio. Several vendors publish no price at all. Haniya is $100 a month per location with no setup fee.

Do AI phone ordering companies charge setup fees or lock you into contracts?

Some do. Slang.ai contracts renew automatically, need 30 days notice before the term ends, and fees paid are non-refundable. Hostie waives setup only on an annual contract. Kea lists a $5,998 annual fee. Haniya has no setup fee and no contract.

Can an AI phone host get the order paid before the kitchen cooks it?

Yes. Haniya texts the guest a pay link and the ticket only reaches the kitchen once it is paid. If the guest does not pay, she follows up. At our own restaurant 15 of 19 phone pay links were paid, most of them after the call had ended.

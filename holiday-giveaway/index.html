    If you win

    How winning works

    We draw and message you

    We pick each day's winner at random the next day, announce them on that day's post, and send a direct message from @azrestaurantpartners.

    Reply within 48 hours to claim your prize, or we draw another winner.

    Claim it your way

    When we message you, we send the prize details and the short terms for claiming it. Say yes and the business that gave the prize helps you book or pick it up.

    Watch out for fake accounts. We only message winners from @azrestaurantpartners. We will never ask you for money, card details, a password or a code.

    Official rules

    The full rules

    Who can enter
    • You must be 18 or older and live in the Greater Houston area.
    • Your Instagram account must be real, and public or able to show us that you follow both accounts.
    • Owners and staff of a business cannot win the prize their own business gives.
    Dates and times

    The giveaway runs from 12:00 AM on December 1, 2026 to 11:59 PM on January 1, 2027, Central Time. There is a separate draw for each day.

    Each day's entries close at 11:59 PM Central Time that day. Comments after that do not count for that day.

    Entries
    • Comment on that day's prize post: 1 entry. Tag a friend in a comment on the same post: 1 bonus entry. The most any person can have for one day is 2 entries.
    • You must follow @azrestaurantpartners and that day's business when the winner is drawn.
    • Extra comments, fake accounts, bots or bought followers do not count, and we may remove those entries.
    • Sharing to your Story is welcome and helps your friends find us. It does not add entries.
    • Thanksgiving bonus: commenting and tagging a friend on our November 26 Thanksgiving post gives 1 bonus entry in the Day 1 draw.
    Prizes
    • Each day has its own prize, worth at least $100, given by the business named on that day's post. Some days have more than one winner, and that day's post says how many.
    • Day 1, December 1: a $250 catering party from Wok & Karahi in Spring, TX. Use it on a Wok & Karahi catering order within 30 days of winning. Book your date with Wok & Karahi ahead of time.
    • Prizes have no cash value, cannot be sold or swapped for cash, and any unused balance is not paid out.
    • The business giving a prize is responsible for honoring it. If a prize cannot be given, we will replace it with one of equal or greater value.
    • One prize per person for the whole giveaway, so more people get to win.
    Choosing and contacting winners

    Winners are picked at random from all eligible entries for that day. We announce the winner on that day's post and message them from @azrestaurantpartners.

    If a winner does not reply within 48 hours, cannot meet these rules, or does not accept the prize terms, we draw another winner for that day.

    Prize terms

    Each prize comes with short claim terms that we send to the winner privately when we message them. Accepting a prize means agreeing to those terms. A winner who does not want to can pass, and we draw another winner.

    Your information

    We only use your Instagram name and comment to run the giveaway. We share a winner's name and contact details only with the business giving their prize, so they can hand it over. We never sell your information.

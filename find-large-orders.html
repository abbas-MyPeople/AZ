How it works

It starts from your actual restaurant

Not a generic list. We read your public menu, cuisine, hours and catering setup, so a tournament that needs family trays ranks differently than an office that wants weekday lunch.

It ranks, it doesn't just list

Anyone can find nearby offices. The useful question is which five deserve your next thirty minutes — scored on likely order size, fit, timing, distance and whether there's a public way to reach them.

Everything shows its sources

Each opportunity links to where we found it. We don't invent attendance numbers, headcounts, contact names or dates. If something isn't published, it says so.

Common questions

Is it really free, and do I have to sign up?

Free, and no. No signup, no email, no card. You get the whole result. We'd rather you find out whether it's useful before we ask you for anything.

Where does the information come from?

Public sources only — business listings, organization websites, public event and venue calendars. Every opportunity shows its sources so you can check it yourself.

What counts as a $500 order?

Roughly a tray-level group order — an office lunch for twenty, a team meal, a family gathering at a tournament. The headline describes the type of opportunity we look for, not a guaranteed amount. We never put a fake dollar figure on an individual lead.

Will you contact these businesses for me?

Not from this tool. It shows you the public route in — a phone number, an organizer page, the right role to ask for. Doing the outreach and building the catering funnel around it is what we do as a paid engagement.

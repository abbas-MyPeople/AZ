Toast vs DoorDash for restaurants: Toast Local, Toast Delivery and DoorDash compared

Toast and DoorDash are not the same kind of product. Toast is your POS, and its Toast Local app lets guests order from nearby Toast restaurants with no commission, so you only pay card processing (Toast support, updated Aug 26, 2026, checked Nov 2026). DoorDash is a marketplace that lists 15%, 25% or 30% commission on delivery and 6% on pickup (DoorDash merchant products, checked Nov 2026).

Most Toast restaurants use both: DoorDash to reach new guests, and Toast's own ordering, Toast Local and Toast Delivery for guests who already know them.

Plain words first

Four things owners mix up

ToastThe point-of-sale system: the register, kitchen screens and payments. It also sells online ordering for your own website.
Toast LocalToast's guest app, formerly Toast Takeout. Guests in the U.S. browse and order from nearby restaurants that use Toast. Restaurants pay only card processing on those orders.
Toast DeliveryToast Delivery Services sends a driver for orders placed on your Toast ordering page or app. The drivers come from Uber Direct or DoorDash Drive, paid per trip.
DoorDashThe marketplace app where guests find you, and you pay a commission. DoorDash also sells Drive (delivery only) and DashOS, its platform for ordering, reservations, loyalty and marketing.

Toast Local and Toast Delivery definitions from Toast support, updated Aug 26, 2026 and Toast support, updated Nov 13, 2026. DashOS from DoorDash, Sep 30, 2026. All checked Nov 2026.

What to do

How to use both without paying twice

  1. Check the Toast Local setting.In Toast Web, under Takeout and delivery, then Toast order sources. If it is off, guests in the app see you as closed.
  2. Turn on Toast Delivery for your own page.Pick Uber Direct or DoorDash Drive and set the guest delivery charge.
  3. Keep DoorDash for reach.Choose the lowest plan that still gives you the visibility you need, and watch which guests come back.
  4. Move regulars to your page.Add your ordering link to bags, receipts and your Google listing. Our guide to building a guest list without delivery apps shows the steps.
  5. Read your Toast contract before adding more.Toast is being sued by the FTC over fees, which Toast disputes. See what the suit alleges and what a POS really costs. If Toast is not the right POS, compare the alternatives and the best restaurant POS systems.

Questions owners ask

Toast vs DoorDash: what is the difference?

Toast is a POS with its own online ordering and the Toast Local guest app. DoorDash is a delivery marketplace that charges a commission. Most Toast restaurants use both.

Does Toast Local charge a commission?

No. Toast's support page says restaurants only pay the card processing fees on Toast Local orders, and contrasts that with other apps charging 20% to 30%.

Toast Local vs DoorDash: which brings more orders?

DoorDash has far more guests looking for food, so it usually brings more new orders. Toast Local costs less per order but reaches guests already using Toast's app.

Toast delivery vs DoorDash: which costs less?

On your own Toast page, a 4-mile trip costs $6.99 with Uber Direct or $7.49 with DoorDash Drive, before card processing, per Toast. On DoorDash Marketplace you pay 15% to 30% of the order instead.

Does Toast use DoorDash for delivery?

Toast Delivery Services uses Uber Direct or DoorDash Drive. Toast made Uber Direct its default in December 2024, and restaurants already on DoorDash Drive could stay.

Toast app vs DoorDash: should I list on both?

Yes for most restaurants. Keep DoorDash for new guests and turn on Toast Local and your own ordering page for regulars.

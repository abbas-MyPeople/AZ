Square Squaremates vs Toast IQ: which POS AI agent does more for a restaurant?

Square's AI agent, Squaremates, became available to all U.S. Square sellers on November 12, 2026 at no extra cost. It builds schedules, finds lapsed guests and drafts win-back offers, bulk-edits the menu, and asks for your approval before it acts (Square press release, Nov 12, 2026, checked Nov 2026). Toast IQ's agents do similar jobs in two paid bundles, reported at $60 a month plus $9 per employee for Team and from $699 a month for Grow, and were in early access with select customers when we last checked (Nation's Restaurant News, Oct 7, 2026; Toast help centre: Toast IQ Agents Overview, checked Oct 2026).

Neither is a reason to switch POS. Use the one you already pay for, and decide who on your team acts on what it suggests.

The honest part

What neither agent does for you

  • Someone still decides. Both default to suggesting. A schedule nobody publishes and a win-back offer nobody approves save nothing. Name one person who reviews the suggestions twice a week.
  • They only see their own POS. Squaremates reads Square data and Toast IQ reads Toast data. Neither sees your DoorDash orders, your phone calls or a review on Google unless those already flow into the POS.
  • They need a before number. Write down last month's overtime hours, lapsed-guest count and food cost before you turn anything on. Without it you cannot tell what the agent earned.
  • Switching POS for an agent rarely pays. New hardware, new processing terms and retraining cost more than any agent saves. See what leaving Toast involves and Toast alternatives.

Questions owners ask

What is Square Squaremates?

Square's AI business agent, generally available to U.S. sellers since November 12, 2026. It can propose staff schedules, find lapsed customers and build win-back offers, bulk-edit menu items and, in beta, prepare supplier reorders. It asks for approval by default.

How much does Squaremates cost?

Square says Squaremates, Square MCP, Square CLI and Squaremates Apps are available to U.S. sellers at no additional cost. Your Square plan and processing fees still apply.

How much does Toast IQ cost?

Nation's Restaurant News reported Toast IQ Team at $60 a month plus $9 per employee and Toast IQ Grow from $699 a month (Oct 7, 2026). Toast's own pricing page blocks automated reading, so confirm current prices with Toast in writing.

Is Squaremates better than Toast IQ?

On price, Squaremates costs nothing extra while Toast IQ is paid. On features, Toast IQ adds review, search and phone agents that Squaremates did not list at launch. The better one is the one on the POS you already run.

Do these AI agents make changes on their own?

By default, no. Squaremates asks for approval unless you let it handle simpler tasks. Toast's help page says its agents send recommendations and do not change anything in Toast on their own.

Does Clover have an AI agent like Squaremates?

Not as of November 2026. Clover added an AI Website Builder and AI-answer tools for online stores on November 10, 2026. Clover restaurants use connected outside tools for scheduling and marketing.

Should I switch POS to get an AI agent?

Rarely. Hardware, processing terms and retraining usually cost more than an agent saves. Use the agent your POS offers, or connect outside tools to the POS you have.

McDonald's and Uber link loyalty and delivery: what it means for independent restaurants

On November 9, 2026, McDonald's and Uber said Uber will be the preferred delivery partner for orders placed in the McDonald's app in 14 markets, including the U.S., with those orders fulfilled mainly through Uber Direct, Uber's white-label delivery service (McDonald's and Uber release, Nov 9, 2026, checked Nov 2026). Rewards members can now trade points for Uber ride discounts in select markets, and earning points on Uber Eats orders is planned with no date.

The lesson for an independent owner: the biggest chain keeps the guest in its own app and rents the driver. You can do the same thing at your size.

The facts

What McDonald's and Uber announced

Where14 markets: the U.S., Australia, Belgium, Canada, France, Germany, Ireland, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland and the U.K.
DeliveryUber Eats becomes the preferred integrated delivery partner inside the McDonald's app in participating markets, including the U.S. Orders are fulfilled mainly through Uber Direct, built with Deliverect, a company that connects ordering channels to the kitchen.
Live nowFrom November, MyMcDonald's Rewards members in select markets can redeem points for discounts on Uber rides, as a seasonal offer in the McDonald's app.
PlannedEarning MyMcDonald's Rewards points on eligible McDonald's orders placed through Uber Eats. No launch date was given.

From the McDonald's and Uber release, Nov 9, 2026 and Restaurant Dive, Nov 9, 2026, both checked Nov 2026. Restaurant Dive describes Uber as already the preferred partner; the release uses future tense, so treat timing per market as unconfirmed.

Why it matters

Three things this tells a small restaurant

  • Own the order, rent the driver. McDonald's wants guests ordering in its own app, where it keeps the guest's details and the menu it controls. Uber supplies the driver. White-label delivery means the guest orders from you and a third-party driver brings it, with no marketplace commission on the food.
  • Loyalty is crossing apps. Points earned at one brand are being spent on rides at another. A guest's habit now forms wherever the rewards are easiest to use. If your loyalty only works at the register, it is competing with programs that work everywhere.
  • The marketplace still finds new guests. McDonald's did not leave the delivery apps. It uses them for reach and its own app for repeat orders. That split is the useful model, more than the size of the deal.

This part is our reading. Neither company said anything about independent restaurants or loyalty tools for other merchants.

What to do

Five steps before the holidays

  1. Count your repeat delivery guests.Look at the last 60 days on each app. Guests who ordered three or more times are the ones to invite to your own page.
  2. Put your own ordering link first.On Google, your website and every receipt. If your POS has online ordering, switch it on. The best restaurant POS guide shows which systems include it, and POS cost shows what it adds.
  3. Turn on white-label delivery.Price it per trip and decide how much of the fee the guest pays. Many ordering systems let you set a guest delivery charge.
  4. Make rewards work on your page.Give a reason to order direct: points, a free side on the third order, or first access to specials. Our read on what loyalty programs return sets the bar.

Questions owners ask

What did McDonald's and Uber announce?

On November 9, 2026, they said Uber will be the preferred delivery partner for orders in the McDonald's app in 14 markets, including the U.S., fulfilled mainly through Uber Direct. Rewards members can redeem points for Uber ride discounts in select markets.

Can I earn McDonald's rewards points on Uber Eats?

Not yet. The companies said they plan to let guests earn MyMcDonald's Rewards points on eligible McDonald's orders through Uber Eats, but gave no launch date.

Does Uber Eats have a loyalty program for restaurants?

The McDonald's deal is a custom partnership. Neither company announced loyalty tools for other restaurants. Independent owners can run their own rewards on their own ordering page.

What is Uber Direct?

Uber's white-label delivery service. A guest orders on the restaurant's own app or site and an Uber courier delivers it, paid per trip with no marketplace commission. Compare it in Uber Direct vs DoorDash Drive.

Is McDonald's leaving DoorDash?

The announcement covers delivery ordered inside the McDonald's app. It did not say McDonald's is leaving any delivery marketplace.

What should an independent restaurant copy?

Keep repeat guests ordering on your own page, use white-label delivery for the drive, give a reason to order direct, and stay on the apps for new guests.

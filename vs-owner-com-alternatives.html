Owner.com alternatives for independent restaurants

The main Owner.com alternatives are ChowNow, Menufy, Popmenu, Square Online and BentoBox, now part of Clover. Menufy has the lowest published flat price, $149 a month on a 12-month term. ChowNow does not list a guest fee. Owner.com charges guests a 5% order support fee on both of its plans, so the fee your guests pay is the first thing to compare.

What Owner.com gives you

Owner.com in one paragraph

Owner.com bundles a website, online ordering, a branded app, automated SEO pages, loyalty, email and text marketing and catering orders. Flex is $249 a month plus a 5% restaurant fee per order. Flat is $499 a month with no restaurant fee, and Owner.com says it suits restaurants above $5,000 a month in online sales. On both plans guests pay a 5% order support fee. It is month-to-month with no cancellation fee, includes setup and 24/7 support, and integrates with Clover, Toast, Square and Lightspeed.

Owners search for alternatives because of the guest fee, because $499 is a lot at low volume, or because they already have a website they like and only want ordering.

The math

What Owner.com costs at your volume

Straight arithmetic from Owner.com's published prices. Put in your own monthly online sales.

$3,000 a monthFlex: $249 + $150 = $399. Flat: $499. Guests pay a further $150 in order fees either way.
$5,000 a monthFlex: $249 + $250 = $499, the same as Flat. This is the break-even Owner.com itself names. Guests pay $250.
$10,000 a monthFlex: $749. Flat: $499. Guests pay $500 in order fees.
ChowNow per orderOn a $30 order, 2.95% + $0.29 is $1.18. Ask ChowNow whether that replaces your card processing or comes on top of it.

Questions owners ask

What is the best alternative to Owner.com?

For ordering only, ChowNow and Menufy. Menufy lists $149 a month on a 12-month term or $179 month-to-month with no per-order commission. ChowNow starts at $229 to $249 a month plus 2.95% and 29 cents per transaction. For a website and marketing first, Popmenu. On Square, try Square Online's free plan first.

Does Owner.com charge guests a fee?

Yes. Owner.com's pricing page says guests pay a 5% order support fee on both plans.

How much does Owner.com cost?

Flex is $249 a month plus 5% per order. Flat is $499 a month with no restaurant fee. Both are month-to-month with no cancellation fee.

When is Owner.com Flat cheaper than Flex?

Above $5,000 a month in online sales. At $5,000 both cost $499; at $10,000 Flex is $749 and Flat is $499.

Is AZ cheaper than Owner.com?

We do not publish a price list, so we will not claim that. We take no per-order percentage from you or your guests, and you keep what we build.

Owner.com vs Popmenu for independent restaurants

Owner.com is built to grow direct online orders: ordering, an app and loyalty are included, from $249 a month plus 5% or $499 flat, and guests pay a 5% fee. Popmenu is built around the website, menu and marketing, at about $159 to $449 a month billed yearly, with online ordering as an extra-cost add-on. Takeout-heavy restaurants usually lean Owner.com; dine-in restaurants that want a better site lean Popmenu.

Questions owners ask

What is the difference between Owner.com and Popmenu?

Owner.com includes online ordering, a branded app and loyalty and is aimed at growing direct online orders; guests pay a 5% fee. Popmenu centres on the website, menu and marketing, with online ordering sold as an extra-cost add-on.

Which is cheaper, Owner.com or Popmenu?

On plan price alone Popmenu starts lower: about $159 a month billed yearly against Owner.com's $249 plus 5% per order. Popmenu's ordering add-on costs extra, so compare total cost with ordering included.

Does Popmenu charge a commission?

Its plans are flat monthly prices. Online ordering is a separate add-on and third-party summaries disagree on its fees, so ask for them in writing.

Does Owner.com have AI phone answering?

Owner.com's AI phone ordering page was a waitlist in October 2026.

Which works with Clover?

Owner.com lists a native Clover integration. We could not confirm Popmenu's POS list on its own pages.

AI receptionist for small business: what it costs and what it can do

An AI receptionist costs a small business between $0 and about $500 a month in October 2026. Most owners land between $79 and $150 a month for a few hundred calls. A human answering service costs more: Ruby starts at $250 a month for 50 minutes. A full-time receptionist costs about $38,000 a year in wages alone (BLS median, 2025).

The price matters less than one question: does it only answer and take messages, or does it finish the job? That means booking the slot, taking the order or getting the payment.

Three levels

What an AI receptionist can actually do

Vendors use the same word for very different products. Sort them into three levels before you compare prices.

Level 1: answerPicks up every call, answers hours, location, pricing and common questions from a knowledge base you write, takes a message and texts or emails it to you. Every vendor above does this.
Level 2: bookPuts the appointment on your calendar (Google, Outlook or a booking tool), sends a confirmation text and reminders. Smith.ai, Goodcall and My AI Front Desk all sell this.
Level 3: transactTakes the order into your point of sale, collects payment, and starts the work. Maple Pro and Serviio do this for restaurants. AZ's phone host does it and also holds the ticket until the guest has paid.

So what do you do? Write down the five calls you get most. If most of them end with "can you book me" or "I want to order", a level 1 product will save you time but will not make you money. Buy for the level your calls need.

The real cost

How to work out your monthly bill

  1. Count your calls. Your phone carrier's call log shows inbound calls per month. Count missed calls separately: those are the ones you are paying to catch.
  2. Match the billing unit. Smith.ai bills per call, Goodcall per unique caller, My AI Front Desk per minute, Serviio per order. A shop with 300 short calls from 120 regulars pays very differently on each.
  3. Add setup time. Self-serve tools need you to write the answers. Budget a few evenings. Enterprise tiers and done-for-you services write them for you.
  4. Add the integrations. Calendar, POS or CRM connections sit on higher tiers on most price pages. Check yours is on the plan you priced.
  5. Compare against the missed sale. One booked job or one large order a week often covers a $150 plan. If calls rarely turn into money, a voicemail-to-text service may be enough.
The honest part

AI receptionist vs a person

Where the AI wins

  • Answers at 2 a.m., on holidays and during your busiest hour, every time.
  • Costs a fraction of a person: $79 to $150 a month against an $18.27 median hourly wage (BLS, May 2025).
  • Never forgets to log the call. Most tools keep a recording and transcript.
  • Handles several calls at once.

Where a person still wins

  • Upset callers, complaints and anything with legal or medical weight.
  • Calls where trust closes the sale, like a $5,000 job estimate.
  • Unusual requests the knowledge base never covered.
  • Heavy accents and noisy lines still cause misheard names. We see it on our own line.

Questions owners ask

How much does an AI receptionist cost for a small business?

Published prices in October 2026 run from free to about $500 a month. Smith.ai has a free 25-call plan and a $150 Pro plan. Goodcall starts at $79 for 100 unique callers. My AI Front Desk is $99 a month for 200 minutes. Restaurant tools like Maple ($150 to $350) and Serviio (2% per order) price differently.

Is an AI receptionist cheaper than an answering service?

Usually, yes. Ruby, a human answering service, starts at $250 a month for 50 minutes and reaches $1,725 for 500 minutes. AI plans with a few hundred calls cost $79 to $150. A person still handles upset callers and high-stakes calls better.

Can an AI receptionist book appointments?

Yes. Smith.ai, Goodcall and My AI Front Desk all book into calendars such as Google or Outlook and send confirmation texts. Check that the calendar connection is included in the plan you are pricing, since some vendors keep integrations on higher tiers.

Can an AI receptionist take orders and payments?

Some can. Maple Pro takes restaurant orders into the POS with payment on the call, and Serviio submits orders to the POS for 2% per order. AZ's phone host builds the order, texts a pay link and sends the ticket to the kitchen only after it is paid.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

Many will, and most callers care more about getting a fast, correct answer than about who gives it. On our own line some callers still hang up during the greeting. A short greeting, quick answers and a clear way to leave a message keep hang-ups down.

How long does it take to set up an AI receptionist?

Self-serve tools can answer basic questions within a day once you write your answers. Getting it to handle your real calls well takes longer. At AZ we set it up in person and review every call with you until it handles your real calls well.

Sunday vs Toast pay at table

If you run Toast, its own QR pay at table and Mobile Order & Pay come with the core POS subscription, with no extra feature fee. Sunday is an add-on at $199 or $299 a month that plugs into Toast, Clover, Lightspeed, NCR and Micros, and adds bill splitting by item, suggested tips and review prompts. Start with Toast's built-in version; add Sunday if you want those extras or run more than one POS.

Plain words first

Two ways to pay at the table

Pay the bill on your phone. The guest eats, scans a code on the table or receipt, sees the check and pays. Staff never carry a card machine. Sunday is built around this, and Toast does it with a QR code printed on the receipt.

Order and pay on your phone. The guest orders from the table and the kitchen gets the ticket straight away. Toast calls this Mobile Order & Pay. It can run as a tab (order rounds, pay at the end) or pay-as-you-go (pay each time you order). Sunday's Standard plan also lists ordering.

Plan for this

What changes when guests pay on their phone

Tips can drop. At our proof restaurant, fewer dine-in guests tipped after we moved to pay-first ordering. We now size the tip buttons to the bill. Sunday sells suggested tips for the same reason.

Card-not-present rates. Toast processes phone payments at your keyed rate, which is usually higher than a tapped card. Check your merchant agreement before you switch every table.

Staff still matter. Someone has to explain the code to the first guests each night and handle a failed card. Plan a one-line staff script.

Questions owners ask

Does Toast have pay at table?

Yes. Toast's help centre says Toast Pay prints a QR code on the receipt with "View check and pay now", and that mobile payments and digital menus are included in the core POS subscription with no extra feature fee. Payments run at your card-not-present rate.

How much does Sunday cost?

Sunday's pricing page lists Standard at $199 a month and Premium at $299 a month, with optional onboarding at $299 and QR codes at $5 each. Transaction fees are not published.

Does Sunday work with Toast?

Yes. Toast lists Sunday in its integration marketplace. Toast's article says a Sunday representative contacts you within one business day and onboarding takes three to four weeks.

Does Sunday work with Clover?

Sunday's FAQ lists Toast, Clover, Lightspeed, NCR and Micros as supported.

Can guests split the bill?

Sunday splits by item, by number of people or by custom amount. Toast's group ordering puts everyone at a table on a single check with their names on the ticket.

Does paying at the table hurt tips?

It can. We saw fewer dine-in guests tip after moving to pay-first at our own restaurant, and fixed it partly by sizing tip buttons to the bill.

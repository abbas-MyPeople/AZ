## C. How to read this for "shifting the needle"

- **🔴 rows are the growth list** — capabilities you've already *paid for* by building them at the flagship, but AZ isn't selling. Turning these into named offers is pure upside (no new build). ✅ **#9 (closed-loop AEO growth engine) is now productized** as the flagship recurring-retainer offer on `details.html`. Remaining priority, reordered 2026-08-17 by *what can be sold with zero new build*: **#18 trust-document monitoring** (universal to every restaurant, cheap, and the flagship's own 592-day-stale certificates are the story), then **#3's per-dish demand** (retroactive, so it produces an insight in the first client meeting), then **#8 MCP connector** (now live — but sell it as "answers for itself inside AI tools", never as AI discovery), then #10 chat, then #17 CDP + receipt-scan spin-wheel.
- **🟠 rows are the proof list** — marketed but thin. The fix isn't more building; it's *surfacing the flagship proof* (the live voice number, the 403→AI-readable before/after, the true-net reconciliation). This is the AZ site's own #1 conversion gap. See [`case-study-wok-and-karahi.md`](case-study-wok-and-karahi.md).
- **⚪ rows are the honesty list** — keep them, but never let the site imply flagship proof that doesn't exist.

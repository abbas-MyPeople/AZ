The 2-second test

What a hook has to do for an owner

MOMENT

A moment they've lived

A Friday rush, a ringing phone, a table that left, a statement from the app. Not a feature.

SPECIFIC

A time, a name or a number

"7:14", "since August", "$26.98". Specific reads as true.

ONE FRAME

Readable with sound off

Seven words or fewer on screen, one picture that carries it.

TENSION

Something is about to be lost

An order, a guest, a table, a night. The next 2 seconds have to answer it.

# Artist commission brief (post on Upwork / ArtStation)

3D artist for a reusable character and set kit (restaurant explainer reels, Blender)

We make short vertical explainer reels about how our restaurant runs. We need a one-time kit of original, production-quality assets that we will animate ourselves in Blender for many reels. Style: stylized realism, warm and human, soft PBR materials, believable proportions. Not chibi, not low-poly toys. Please share 3 portfolio pieces closest to this.

CHARACTERS (7), rigged and animation-ready
- Restaurant owner (South Asian, 40s), chef (wok cook, apron, cap), runner, counter host (hijab), a dad caller, two kids (about 6 and 9)
- Real hands with articulated fingers; facial rig with blinks, smile, and mouth shapes for talking (A, E, O, M, F at minimum)
- Humanoid skeleton compatible with Rigify; clothing that moves believably
- Around 30-50k triangles each, 2K PBR textures
- A front, side and back turnaround sheet for approval before modelling

SETS (5)
- A halal Chinese, Indian and Pakistani restaurant: dining room of 12 tables (floor plan provided), an open wok line with burners, a pickup counter, a storefront with sign
- A residential street at night (houses, street lights) leading to the restaurant
- The caller's home: kitchen and living area
- The owner's small back office
- Correct real-world scale in meters

PROPS
- Wall phone with cord, smartphones, a counter tablet on a stand, a ticket printer with paper, takeout bags, woks, plates of our dishes (chowmein, biryani, karahi), a wall screen
- Every screen as a separate material slot so we can drive its content

DELIVERY
- Blender files (version 4.x or later), organized collections, plus GLB exports
- Milestones: 1) concept and turnarounds, 2) characters, 3) sets and props
- Full commercial rights to all work, as work for hire
- Please quote by milestone and give your timeline.

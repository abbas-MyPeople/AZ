Title

We let a tiny Google ad turn itself off. Attribution for a 5-person restaurant.

Text

Every order on our site carries where it came from (search ad, map, direct, phone, table). Ran a small Google search ad and watched which clicks became food. Half the spend brought zero orders. The system paused that half automatically and texted me why. Agencies sell this for thousands. The core is one rule: a dollar you cannot trace to an order is a dollar you do not spend. If your agency cannot tell you which of last month's orders came from the ad, nobody can.

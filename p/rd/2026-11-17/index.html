Title

Seven languages at the table. The seventh was asked for on a Tuesday and live on Friday.

Text

A guest asked whether our table menu spoke her language. It did not. By Friday the table menu, the phone host and the pay screen did. Kitchen ticket stayed in English for the cooks. In Spring, TX seven languages covers most of the neighborhood. Orders from guests who used to point at pictures went up. The lesson for me was less about languages and more about owning the stack: when a guest asks for something, the answer can be yes by Friday instead of a vendor ticket.

Title

Thirty days of tracking which of our posts brought owners to talk to us. Numbers posts won.

Text

We posted daily for a month about restaurant margins across a few platforms and tracked which posts led to an owner actually reaching out. Posts with our own numbers (swipe fee on tips, more than 1 in 4 calls after close, 15 of 19 pay links) brought conversations. Opinion posts brought likes. Nobody cared that it is AI; they cared about the fee on the tip. Reddit answers with no links brought the most serious people. Sharing in case anyone else is doing this.

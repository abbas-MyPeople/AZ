Title

Upsell math: our automated phone host gets about 1 in 3 callers to add one item. Humans do it on Tuesday and forget on Friday.

Text

Numbers from our place. The host offers one add-on per call (naan, drink, dessert), in whatever language the caller used. About 1 in 3 say yes. Our best server did about the same on a quiet night and close to zero on a slammed one, which is when the volume is. Not an argument against servers. An argument that the phone should not be on the floor at all. Our team runs the room, the system runs the phone.

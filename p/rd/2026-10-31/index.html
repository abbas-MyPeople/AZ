Title

Left Google to run my family's restaurant. Built the "head office" a chain has, for one store. What that actually means.

Text

Not a pitch, a breakdown of what the big chains have that independents do not, because I kept hearing "AI" and that is not it. What they have is a data layer: every order from every channel (phone, counter, web, apps) lands in one register with a time and a source. Once you have that you can answer: which ad paid, which regular vanished, what tonight looks like. What independents have: five tablets, a POS, a phone and a notebook. The owner is the data layer, at midnight. I built the layer for our 5-person restaurant first. Phone host first (the phone was where we lost the most orders), then payments, then attribution, then a morning report. Happy to go into any piece.

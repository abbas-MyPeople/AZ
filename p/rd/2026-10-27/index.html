Swipe fees are charged on the sales tax and tips, not just the sale. Did the math on our books.

Text

Restaurant owner. I always assumed the ~3% card fee applied to our food sales. Went through the statements line by line: it is charged on the full ticket, which includes the sales tax we collect for the state and the tips we pass on to staff. So a chunk of our processing cost is a fee on money that was never ours. Two things that helped: one payment per order (we moved to pay-first so there is no second swipe or chargeback mess), and knowing the number exists. The Credit Card Competition Act got reintroduced in January; whatever you think of it, worth knowing what it does.

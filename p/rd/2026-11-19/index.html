Title

Pay-first in a restaurant: what broke, what did not. Two months of data.

Text

We flipped the restaurant default (cook, serve, then ask for money) to pay-first on every channel: QR at the table, pay link on the phone, prepaid on the app. What did not break: 317 of 317 app orders prepaid. 100 of 101 dine-in tables paid from their phone. Dine-and-dash zero. What we had to handle: one regular who wants to pay at the counter (staff approve it), and making the pay link chase itself (15 of 19 unpaid links paid without a human call). Every other business you use asks first. Restaurants are the odd one.

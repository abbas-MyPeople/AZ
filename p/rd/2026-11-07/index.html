Title

Catering after hours: the call comes at 9 PM from an office manager planning tomorrow. How we stopped losing it.

Text

We noticed the pattern in our call log: catering requests cluster right around and after close. Office managers plan tomorrow's lunch at night. We were closed, it went to voicemail, they called the next place. Fix: after-hours calls are answered by an automated host that can take a catering request (headcount, time, items) and send a pay link. Follow-up the next morning is automatic. We also built a simple list of offices, churches and schools within a few miles and reach out. Boring, works. If you do nothing else: look at what time your catering calls arrive.

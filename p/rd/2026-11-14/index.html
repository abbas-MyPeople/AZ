Two months of moving orders off 30% delivery apps and making every order prepaid. What changed in our margins.

Text

Restaurant, Spring TX. Two changes: our own ordering page for pickup and delivery, and pay-first on every channel (table QR, phone pay link, app, web). What happened, from our books: - 100% of our direct orders are self-serve now. The delivery apps are still on, they are just no longer the front door. - 317 of 317 app orders prepaid. - 15 of 19 unpaid phone pay links got paid without anyone calling the guest. - About 1 in 3 phone callers takes the one add-on offered. The apps take up to 30% of an order. Every order that moves to your own page keeps that. Happy to break down any line.

Title

We tracked every missed call for a month. More than 1 in 4 came in after close.

Text

Small family place in Spring, TX. We put call logging on the line for 30 days to see what we were losing. Result: more than 1 in 4 inbound calls arrived after close or before open. Most were pickup orders for the next day and a few were catering (office managers planning lunch at 9 PM). All of them went to voicemail and almost none called back. We changed two things: the phone is now answered after hours (an automated host, takes the order, sends a pay link) and catering requests get a callback the next morning automatically. If you have not measured this, pull your carrier's call log for a month. The number surprised us.

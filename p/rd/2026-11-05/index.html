Delivery apps take up to 30%. In Texas, cities are not allowed to cap it (SB 911). What we did instead.

Text

NYC capped delivery app fees at 15%. Texas passed SB 911 which stops any city here from doing the same. So in Houston the apps still take up to 30% and there is no cap coming. We stopped waiting. Built ordering on our own site, made it the default on the menu, the phone and at the table. 100% of direct orders are now self-serve. The apps are still on for people who insist, they are just no longer the front door. The bigger loss than the 30% was the customer list: the app keeps the name and phone, you get a bag. Now we have the phone number for next month.

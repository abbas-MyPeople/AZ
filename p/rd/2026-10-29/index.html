We made the kitchen cook nothing until it was paid. 100 of the last 101 dine-in tables were fine with it.

Text

Context: we moved ordering to a QR at the table and the phone to an automated host. Pay-first was the scary part. We expected pushback. What happened over the last 101 tables: 100 ordered and paid from their phone. One regular (70s, comes to chat) ordered from staff and paid at the counter; staff can approve that. Side effects: dine-and-dash went to zero, no more "I will pay on the way out", and the allergy note is typed by the guest and prints word for word, which cut relay mistakes. Happy to answer questions on how guests reacted.

Finishing the Google profile

Read off your live listing on 18 August 2026. Everything below is copy-ready. Do it in the order on this page - the first two changes are worth more than the other eight combined.

One thing before you start. You asked for all ten category slots filled. Google's own 2026 guidance is that categories describe what a business is, not what it wants to rank for, and that padding the list dilutes relevance rather than adding reach. So the list below is ranked: change the primary, add the three core, and take the remaining six only where the service is genuinely something you deliver. Ten accurate categories beat ten aspirational ones, and an inaccurate one can suppress the whole profile.

Categories

One primary and up to nine more. The primary carries most of the weight, so it is the single most valuable field on the profile.

PRIMARY Business management consultant

Carries the most ranking weight of anything on the profile. Replaces the generic Consultant you have now, which is close to unsearchable. It matches what you actually sell - running the operational and technical side of a business - and it is far less saturated in Houston than Marketing agency, which matters because a service-area business with no address is already fighting uphill in the local pack.

CORE Marketing agency

Highest search volume of any category you qualify for. You will not win it outright against established Houston agencies, but as a secondary it captures the owner who searches in those words without staking your primary on the hardest term.

CORE Website designer

Very high local intent and you genuinely do it. 'Website designer near me' is one of the few searches a restaurant owner runs with a credit card already out.

CORE Internet marketing service

Covers the getting-found half of the offer - Google, Maps and AI answers - without the word SEO, which you avoid for good reason.

IF TRUE Software company

Add it. You build custom software when nothing off the shelf fits, and it separates you from every agency that only resells.

IF TRUE Advertising agency

Only if you actually run paid campaigns for clients. If you do not, leave it off - it invites the wrong enquiry and dilutes the rest.

IF TRUE Computer consultant

Fits the POS, integrations and systems work. Lower volume, but very close to what an owner with a broken system types.

IF TRUE Telecommunications service provider

Only because of the AI phone line. Genuinely defensible, but expect calls about phone systems, so add it last.

IF TRUE Business development service

Catering, loyalty and win-back are revenue development, not marketing. Low volume, accurate.

IF TRUE Data analyst

The dashboard and the numbers work. Thin on volume - take it only if you want the tenth slot filled.

How to answer every restaurant call with AI, and what still needs a human

An AI phone host can answer every call the moment it rings, including while you are closed, and handle hours, menu questions, takeout orders with payment and order status on its own. Complaints, refunds, large catering deals and anything about a serious allergy should reach a person. Plan on about three months running the AI alongside your staff before it carries nearly all calls.

The ramp

How to roll it out over three months

No AI phone host knows your restaurant on day one. Treat the first three months as training, with your staff as the safety net.

Write down what staff already say

Hours, parking, the dishes people ask about, halal and spice answers, catering minimums, delivery radius. This becomes the AI's script.

Start with the calls you miss

Send overflow and closed-hours calls to the AI first. Your staff keep the rest. Nothing gets worse, and you learn fast.

Review every call each week

Read the transcripts. Every question it could not answer gets a written answer once, and it uses it from then on.

Turn on ordering with payment

When the AI handles questions well, let it take orders. A pay link by text and a ticket that waits for payment keep it safe.

Widen the share of calls

Move more calls to the AI as the review list shrinks. Owner alerts cover complaints and urgent problems.

Keep a person on the hard calls

Complaints, refunds, allergy confirmations and big catering deals stay with you. After about three months the AI carries nearly all of the rest.

Rules to know

Inbound is simple. Outbound has rules.

Answering calls that guests place to you is ordinary business. Calling guests back with an AI voice is different: in February 2024 the FCC confirmed that AI-generated voices count as "artificial" voices under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act. That means you need the right consent before an AI calls a guest, for example to follow up on a catering quote. Keep AI callbacks to people who asked for them, and keep a record.

Questions owners ask

Can AI answer restaurant phone calls?

Yes. An AI phone host can answer every call, give hours and menu answers, take takeout orders with a text pay link and check order status. Hard calls like complaints and refunds should go to a person.

What restaurant calls still need a human?

Complaints, refunds, serious allergy questions, price negotiation on large catering, and anything the AI has not been taught yet.

How long does it take an AI phone host to learn a restaurant?

Plan on about three months running alongside your staff. Each week you review calls and add answers. After that it handles nearly all routine calls.

Can the AI take payment on the call?

Some vendors take the card on the call. Ours texts a secure pay link while the host stays on the line, and the kitchen ticket prints only once it is paid.

Can an AI call my guests back?

Only with the right consent. The FCC said in 2024 that AI voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA, so follow the same consent rules as any automated call.

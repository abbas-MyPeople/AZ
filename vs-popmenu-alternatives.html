Popmenu alternatives for restaurants

The main Popmenu alternatives are Owner.com for ordering-led growth, BentoBox (now Clover) for design-led websites, and ChowNow or Menufy if you only need commission-free ordering. Popmenu's plans run from about $159 to $449 a month billed yearly, and online ordering is an extra-cost add-on. Owner.com includes ordering but charges guests a 5% fee.

What Popmenu gives you

Popmenu in one paragraph

Popmenu is a website, menu and marketing platform. A pricing tracker captured its plans in July 2026 at Starter $159, Essentials $269 and Premier $449 a month billed yearly; a third-party summary of its pricing page lists $179 and $499 for monthly billing on Starter and Premier. Starter covers the website, interactive menu, Google profile sync and automated email. Essentials adds mass email, social posting and 500 texts. Premier adds 5,000 texts, segments, a monthly calendar of AI-written emails and posts, waitlist and reservations, guest feedback and AI-written review replies. Online ordering and catering are marked extra cost. Its AI phone answering add-on lost its "$149 a month" starting price in July 2026 and is now priced on request.

Popmenu blocks automated reading of its own pricing page, so check every figure on a written quote.

Questions owners ask

What is a good alternative to Popmenu?

Owner.com if you want online ordering, an app and loyalty included. BentoBox, now part of Clover, for a design-led website. ChowNow or Menufy if you only need commission-free ordering added to your current site.

How much does Popmenu cost?

A pricing tracker captured Starter at $159, Essentials at $269 and Premier at $449 a month billed yearly in July 2026. Third-party summaries list $179 and $499 for monthly billing. Online ordering is an extra-cost add-on. Get a written quote.

Does Popmenu include online ordering?

Not in the base plans. Popmenu's pricing page marks online ordering and catering as extra cost, according to third-party summaries of that page.

How much is Popmenu's AI phone answering?

Popmenu removed the "$149 a month" starting price for AI phone answering from its pricing page in July 2026, per a pricing tracker. It is now priced on request.

Is Popmenu or Owner.com better?

Popmenu leans to website, menu and marketing; Owner.com leans to direct online orders with ordering included and a 5% guest fee. See our Owner.com vs Popmenu page for the full comparison.

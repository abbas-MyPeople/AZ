What it means

An engineer on your side of the counter

Most restaurant tech is sold from the outside: a sales call, a login and a help line. A forward-deployed engineer works from the inside. We spend time in your restaurant, find where money and hours are leaking, and fix them in the order that pays back fastest.

Our rule is simple: use what works, customize what fits, build what is missing. Sometimes the right answer is a vendor on our compare pages. Sometimes it is a change to how your staff hand over the table card. Sometimes nothing on the market does the job, so we build it.

The founder, Abbas Zoeb, is a software engineer who has built AI agents for Hard Rock Cafe and runs his family's restaurant, Wok & Karahi in Spring, TX. Everything below runs there first.

Week by week

What the first months look like

First call

You tell us what is hurting

Missed calls, app commissions, slow tables, no repeat guests. We ask about your POS, your menu, your staff and your guests' languages.

Week 1

We watch a real rush

Lunch and dinner on site. We read your POS reports, delivery statements, reviews and phone pattern, and write down every leak with a rough size.

Week 2

You see it working on your menu

Your menu in the ordering app and at the table, and a test call to the phone host using your dishes. You say what is wrong; we change it.

Weeks 3 to 4

One door goes live at a time

Usually the table QR or online ordering first. Table cards, a short staff script and the tip setup go in with it. We watch every order the first week.

Months 1 to 2

Running cost only while we tune it

For the first two months you only cover what the technology costs to run. The AI host answers your calls while we review every one, teach it your answers and widen what it handles each week.

Month 3 on

We earn only on orders we bring you

No setup fee, no contract. You get a morning brief and one text when something needs you. We build what is still missing and review the numbers with you each month.

By department

What gets automated, and what stays with your people

Phone

An AI host answers every call, takes the order, texts a pay link and sends the ticket once paid. It takes catering details, finds a caller's order and answers while you are closed. Your staff still handle anything it passes on.

Front of house

Guests scan, tap their table, order and pay on their phone. Several phones share one cart. Older guests can read the menu in Urdu, Hindi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Arabic or Spanish, or talk to the assistant. Servers spend their time on hospitality.

Kitchen

Only paid orders print. Scheduled pickups print 20 minutes early. The chef gets a 10 AM prep text. Say "Kung Pao is off until 5" once and it disappears from the POS, the site, the table and the phone.

Marketing

Reviews read and replies drafted in your voice, your Google profile tracked, visibility in ChatGPT and Gemini answers measured, guests texted on their own rhythm, catering notes sent to nearby offices, and search ads judged on real profit.

Money

Every order paid before cooking. Card declines explained to the guest with another way to pay. Watchers check every 30 minutes that orders were paid in full, and dispute packs are ready if a guest charges back.

Owner

One dashboard on your phone, a 10 AM brief, an 11 PM recap, and one text when something needs you. Call your own line and the assistant tells you how today is going or turns a dish off, with an undo text.

Questions owners ask

What is a forward-deployed engineer for a restaurant?

An engineer who works inside your restaurant instead of selling you software from outside. They watch your rushes, read your numbers, then set up, change or build the tools that fix what is costing you money, and stay to run them with your staff until they work.

How is that different from a restaurant technology consultant?

A consultant usually hands you a recommendation. A forward-deployed engineer does the work: connects your POS, sets up the phone host and the table QR, writes the staff script, builds what no vendor sells, and watches the results every week.

Will you make me switch POS?

No. We start from the POS you have. Our own restaurant runs on Clover. For Toast or Square we check what the POS allows before we promise anything.

Do you only sell your own tools?

No. Our rule is use what works, customize what fits, build what is missing. If Owner.com, Toast or Maple is the right fit for one job, we set that up. We take no vendor commission, so the recommendation is not bent by a kickback.

How long until the AI phone host handles calls on its own?

We set it up in person, then review every call and teach it your answers. Complaints, refunds and big catering deals still reach you.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in the Houston area and work with independent restaurants across Greater Houston, including Spring, The Woodlands, Katy, Sugar Land and Cypress. Much of the work is remote, with time on site when it helps.

What does it cost?

We do not publish a price list. Every restaurant needs different jobs replaced, so we start with what is hurting and quote that work before anything starts. No vendor commissions, and you keep everything we build.

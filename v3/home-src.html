Questions

What owners ask us

Can AI really replace restaurant staff?

For ordering, payment and the phone, yes. At our restaurant, every table order is placed and paid by the guest on their own phone, and an AI host answers every call. Your cooks and the people who carry food still matter. The order-taking, cashier and phone work no longer needs a person.

Can an AI take phone orders straight into Clover?

Yes. Haniya writes the order into Clover, texts the guest a pay link, and the ticket prints once it's paid. She speaks English, Urdu, Hindi and Spanish and knows regulars by their number.

How much does it cost?

It depends on which jobs you want covered, from just the phone to the whole front of house. Tell us what you want and we price it on that. No long contract.

What if it gets an order wrong?

The guest sees the whole order on their phone and pays only once it's right. Nothing reaches the kitchen until it's paid. Every call is recorded and filed, so you can listen to any of them.

Who owns the guest list?

You do. Every guest who orders direct goes into your own list with their name. Delivery apps keep that from you.

Do I need a new POS?

No. We work with what you have. Our own restaurant runs on Clover.

What happens when the restaurant is closed?

The AI still answers. At our restaurant more than 1 in 4 calls come in while we're closed. She tells the caller when we open, takes the order for later or holds a table.

Before and after

Six jobs. Who did them then, what does them now.

The same problems most independents have: a phone nobody can answer in the rush, apps taking a cut of every order, and an owner who has to be there for anything to happen.

Taking dine-in orders

BeforeA server walked every order to the register.

NowGuests scan the table code, order in their language and pay. Every table order is placed and paid by the guest.

Answering the phone

BeforeCalls rang out in the rush.

NowHaniya, the AI host, answers every call, writes the order into Clover and texts a pay link. 273 real calls in 3 weeks.

The cashier

BeforeA person on the register, and pay at the counter.

NowEvery order is paid before it cooks, 6 in 10 with Apple Pay or Google Pay.

Delivery apps

BeforeThree tablets, orders re-typed into the register, about 30% gone to the app.

NowOne register for every channel. The restaurant's own ordering at its own prices, with 100% of direct orders self-placed.

Marketing

BeforeA website AI assistants couldn't read, and a guest list the apps kept.

NowNamed in about 7 of 10 AI answers to local food questions. 1,988 guests known by name. Reviews 400 → 941, 4.5 stars.

The manager on call

BeforeThe owner had to be in the building.

NowA 10 AM text, alerts only when something needs him, and menu changes by voice. 20 watchers run all day.

What it takes in your restaurant

It ramps. Then it runs.

  • The phone AI runs hybrid: some calls go to Haniya, the rest still go to your staff.
  • We tune her to your menu, your regulars and your rules every week.
  • Table ordering and pay-first go live on day one.

After that

  • The phone is about 99% hands-free.
  • You get one text a morning and an alert only when something needs you.
  • You keep the customers, the data and the phone number.

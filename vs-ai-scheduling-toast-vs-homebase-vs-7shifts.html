AI restaurant scheduling: Toast IQ vs Homebase vs 7shifts

Homebase is the cheapest way to try AI scheduling: its AI Scheduling Assistant comes with the Plus plan at $70 per location a month, and a free plan covers one location with up to 10 staff (Homebase pricing, checked Oct 2026).

Toast's new Scheduling Agent works only on Toast and is in beta. 7shifts builds schedules from POS sales forecasts on any major POS. Scheduling is also where restaurant AI meets the least pushback from staff, so it is a safe first step.

Pick by situation

Which one fits your restaurant

Under 10 staff, one location

Start on Homebase's free plan. Move to Plus only when building the schedule by hand takes you more than an hour a week.

Already on Toast

Ask for the Scheduling Agent beta. Per-employee pricing grows with your crew, so compare it with Homebase Plus at your headcount before you commit.

Why scheduling first

The AI your staff and guests will not fight

Guest-facing AI gets the headlines and the backlash. A Temple University researcher told Restaurant Dive that demand forecasting and inventory tools have drawn the least pushback, and that AI has worked well for scheduling (Restaurant Dive, Oct 7, 2026, checked Oct 2026). Guests never see the schedule, and a manager reviews every draft before it goes out.

The catch: a forecast is only as good as the sales data behind it. If phone orders are written on paper or catering is invoiced outside the POS, the forecast misses the rush. Get every order into one system first.

Questions owners ask

What is the best AI scheduling app for a small restaurant?

For one location on a budget, Homebase: its AI Scheduling Assistant comes with the Plus plan at $70 per location a month, and a free plan covers up to 10 staff. On Toast, try Toast's Scheduling Agent beta. If you want POS sales forecasting across several locations, look at 7shifts.

Is there free restaurant scheduling software?

Yes. Homebase lists a free Basic plan for one location with up to 10 employees, checked October 2026. The AI Scheduling Assistant starts on the paid Plus plan.

How much does Toast IQ Team cost?

Nation's Restaurant News reported on October 7, 2026 that Toast IQ Team starts at $60 a month plus $9 per employee a month. Confirm with Toast, since its own page could not be read automatically.

Does AI scheduling work with Clover?

Homebase lists a Clover integration that syncs employees and sales and tracks labor cost against sales. Toast IQ does not work on Clover. Check 7shifts' integration list for Clover before you sign.

Does AI scheduling actually reduce overtime?

Toast says restaurants in its Scheduling Agent beta averaged 41% fewer overtime hours per employee than those not using it. That is Toast's own figure. Measure your overtime hours for a month before you start so you can check the result yourself.

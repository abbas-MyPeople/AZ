How much does it cost to automate a small business back office?

Automating the back office of a small business with 10 to 15 staff typically costs a few hundred dollars a month in software, using public 2026 prices. That covers scheduling, payroll, an AI phone answerer and bookkeeping help. Full-service bookkeeping is the biggest line: NerdWallet puts typical packages at $200 to $700 a month or more.

The software is cheap. The expensive part is the time to set it up and connect the tools so data flows without someone retyping it.

The hidden cost

Why cheap tools still cost owners their evenings

Each tool above works on its own. The trouble starts between them. Hours from scheduling have to reach payroll. Sales from the POS have to reach the books. A phone order has to reach the kitchen and the guest list. When those handoffs are manual, the owner or a manager retypes numbers every week, and mistakes creep in.

Signs your back office is not really automated

  • You export a spreadsheet from one app to upload into another.
  • The books are a month or more behind.
  • You find out about a problem days later, from a customer.
  • Only one person knows how the reports are made.

What connected looks like

  • Orders, payments and the guest list update each other on their own.
  • One short morning summary tells you yesterday's sales and today's problems.
  • Alerts arrive while there is still time to act.
  • You can change a price or switch an item off from your phone.

Questions owners ask

How much does it cost to automate a small business back office?

Using public 2026 list prices, software for scheduling, payroll, phone answering and bookkeeping help for a 12-person business comes to roughly $550 to $600 a month. Bookkeeping is the largest line at $200 to $700 a month. Setup and connecting the tools is extra time or extra cost.

What back-office tasks should a small business automate first?

Automate the task that eats the most hours each week. For most owners that is answering the phone or keeping the books current, not payroll. Measure the hours saved on the first one before adding the next tool.

Can AI do my bookkeeping?

AI tools can pull bank and card data and suggest categories, which removes most data entry. A person still needs to review the month, fix odd transactions and make tax decisions. NerdWallet puts typical bookkeeping packages at $200 to $700 a month.

How much does payroll software cost for a small business?

Square Payroll lists $35 a month plus $6 per person paid, read October 2026. For 12 staff that is $107 a month. Prices vary by provider and by whether contractors only or full payroll with tax filing is needed.

Is there free scheduling software for small businesses?

Yes. Homebase lists a free Basic plan for one location with up to 10 employees. Paid plans are $30, $70 and $120 per location per month, read October 2026. Above 10 staff you need a paid plan.

Why do automated tools still create work?

Because each tool is automated on its own and the handoffs between them are not. Hours, sales and orders get retyped from one app to another. Connecting the tools so data flows on its own is where most of the saved time comes from.

What owners ask first

How much does it cost?

It's priced by what you want implemented. We learn your problem first, you pick the jobs, from just the phone to the whole front of house, and we price it on that. No long contract.

Do I need a new POS?

No. We work with what you have. Our own restaurant runs on Clover.

Will the phone AI work on day one?

It ramps. For about three months it runs hybrid: some calls go to the AI, the rest still go to your staff while we tune it to your menu and regulars. After that it reaches about 99% hands-free.

Who builds it?

Abbas Zoeb, a former Google software engineer whose family owns Wok & Karahi in Spring, Texas, with AI agents doing the heavy lifting. He stays your point of contact.

Who owns the data and the customers?

You do. Your customers, your data, your website and your phone number stay yours.

Do you disappear after it's set up?

No. The system watches itself all day and alerts us when something breaks. Abbas stays your point of contact.

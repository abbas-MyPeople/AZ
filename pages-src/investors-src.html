The model

Build it in a real restaurant. Then make it a setting.

01

Prove it at home

Every piece runs at Wok & Karahi first, on real guests and real money, before another owner sees it.

02

Ramp, then hands-free

At a new restaurant the phone AI runs hybrid for about three months, with staff taking the calls it doesn't. After that, about 99% hands-free.

03

Configuration, not construction

Each restaurant's menu, hours, languages and rules move into one settings file, so restaurant five costs a fraction of restaurant one.

Restaurants pick the jobs they want replaced and are priced on that. Clover-first: Toast and Square now ship their own voice agents, Clover has no equivalent AI layer, and our flagship runs on Clover.

What investors ask us

How much are you raising?

$1,000,000 to cover 24 months: turning the system into configuration, taking the founding cohort from two restaurants to five, and the first hires.

Is it live, or a demo?

Live, in our own restaurant. Every table order is placed and paid by the guest on their own phone, an AI host answers every call, and 100% of our direct orders arrive with no staff member taking them.

How does the phone AI roll out at a new restaurant?

As a ramp. For about three months it runs hybrid: some calls go to the AI, the rest still go to staff. After that it reaches about 99% hands-free.

Why Clover?

Our flagship runs on Clover, and Clover has no equivalent AI layer of its own. Toast and Square now ship voice agents for their own restaurants, which leaves the rest of the market open.

How to lower your restaurant's credit card processing fees

Published restaurant card rates in November 2026 run from 2.4% + 15 cents to 2.79% + 20 cents for a tapped or swiped card, and up to 3.79% + 20 cents for a typed-in card (Square for Restaurants pricing; SpotOn pricing, checked Nov 2026). Toast and Clover do not publish rates we could read; most Clover pricing is set by the bank or reseller that sells it.

The fastest savings come from three moves: know your real all-in rate, stop typing card numbers, and check whether a paid plan's lower rate beats its monthly fee at your volume.

Seven steps

How to cut what you pay

  1. Find your real rate.Add up every processing fee on last month's statement and divide by your total card sales. That is your effective processing rate. Write it down; every other step is measured against it.
  2. Stop typing card numbers.On Square a typed card costs 3.5% + 15¢ against 2.6% + 15¢ tapped. That is 0.9 points on every phone order you key in. Send a pay link by text instead, or take payment online before the order is made.
  3. Check if a paid plan pays for itself.Square Plus costs $49 a month and saves 0.1 points in person and 0.4 points online. It pays for itself at about $12,250 a month of online card sales ($49 / 0.004), or about $49,000 a month in person ($49 / 0.001). Premium saves a further 0.1 points in person for $100 more, so it needs about $100,000 a month of in-person card sales on top.
  4. Mind the fixed fee on small tickets.15 cents is 1.5% of a $10 coffee order but 0.3% of a $50 dinner. If most tickets are small, a lower fixed fee matters more than a lower percentage.
  5. Read the contract, not just the rate.SpotOn's $0 plan has a two-year minimum and processing minimums. Ask every vendor for term length, early termination fees and whether hardware is tied to the processing. Our guide to leaving a Toast contract shows what that can cost.
  6. Cut chargebacks.A disputed charge costs the sale plus a fee. Itemised receipts, clear descriptors on the guest's statement and quick replies to disputes keep them down. See what a chargeback is.
  7. Decide on surcharges carefully.Passing the fee to guests comes with state law and card-network rules, and guests notice it. Read our surcharge rules guide before you add one.
Coming

What the Visa and Mastercard settlement could change

A revised settlement between merchants and Visa and Mastercard would cut credit card interchange (the fee the card-issuing bank takes) by 10 basis points, or 0.1 points, for five years, and set a 1.25% rate on standard consumer cards for eight years. It would also let merchants decline some higher-cost premium and commercial cards, and add new rights to surcharge or discount by card type (Payments Dive, Nov 17, 2026, checked Nov 2026).

It is not final. The judge granted preliminary approval in June, heard objections on November 16, 2026 and has not ruled. Walmart, the National Retail Federation and the convenience store association are among about 1,000 objectors. If it is rejected, the case goes to trial in 2027.

What to do now: nothing changes on your statement yet. When it does, a flat-rate plan like Square's may not pass the savings to you automatically. Ask your provider how it will handle lower interchange.

Questions owners ask

What are average credit card processing fees for restaurants?

Published rates in November 2026 run from 2.4% + 15 cents to 2.79% + 20 cents for a card that is tapped, dipped or swiped, and 2.9% + 30 cents to 3.3% + 30 cents online on Square. Typed-in cards cost more, 3.45% to 3.79% plus a fixed fee. Your real rate is total fees divided by total card sales.

What is the cheapest credit card processing for a small restaurant?

Among published rates we checked, Square Premium has the lowest in-person rate at 2.4% + 15 cents, but costs $149 a month. At lower volume, Square Free at $0 a month and 2.6% + 15 cents often costs less overall. Do the break-even math on your own card sales.

How can a restaurant lower credit card processing fees?

Measure your effective rate, stop keying card numbers, check whether a paid plan's lower rate beats its fee at your volume, watch the fixed fee on small tickets, read contract terms and cut chargebacks.

What are Toast's credit card processing rates?

Toast's pricing page blocks automated reading, so we could not confirm current rates. Ask Toast for your rate, any monthly fees and the contract term in writing.

What are Clover's processing fees for restaurants?

Clover is sold through banks and resellers, and each sets its own rate. Ask for a written quote showing the rate per card type and any monthly fees.

Will the Visa and Mastercard settlement lower my fees?

If approved, it would cut credit interchange by 0.1 points for five years and set a 1.25% rate on standard consumer cards for eight years. The judge had not ruled as of November 17, 2026. Ask your provider whether lower interchange will reach your rate.

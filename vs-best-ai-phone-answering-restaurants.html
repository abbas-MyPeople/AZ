The shortlist

Best by job, with the reason

1. Maple Pro

Best published flat price with POS ordering

$350 a month or $220 paid yearly. Orders into Toast, Square, Clover and Shift4 Dine, payment over the phone, OpenTable reservations, no per-minute fees. Its $150 Voice plan answers but does not take orders.

2. Serviio

Best with no monthly fee

2% per submitted order, no monthly or setup fee, three-month free trial, no contract. Lists English, Spanish, Chinese, Japanese, Hindi, French and Italian. Does not say it takes payment on the call.

3. Bitebuddy

Best pay per completed order

$1.50 per completed order at list rate. Lists Toast, Square, Clover, Olo, SpotOn and NCR Aloha. Ask what counts as completed.

4. Loman Premium

Best POS coverage with card on the call

Orders into Toast, Clover, Square, SpotOn, Shift4, Aloha and Olo, card on the call, reservations. Price on request; no per-minute fees.

5. Kea

Best flat rate with wallets on the call

Card, Apple Pay and Google Pay on the call, 11+ POS integrations. Flat rate with no per-order cost, price on request.

6. Slang.ai

Best for reservation-led full service

$399 or $599 per location. Books in OpenTable, SevenRooms, Yelp and Resy; Premium switches among 97 languages. Texts an ordering link; no order on the call.

7. Yelp Host

Best if you run Yelp Guest Manager

From $249 a month. Reservations and waitlist by phone; says it handles nearly any language. No orders on the call.

8. AZ

Best when you want it set up and run with you

Takes the order, texts a pay link and prints the ticket only once paid; catering intake; greets in Urdu, Hindi and Spanish. Free for the first two months, then we earn only on orders we bring you. Proven at one restaurant, our own, so it ranks on fit, not scale.

Before you sign

Five questions to ask any vendor

1

What counts as a billable order or call?

Cancelled tickets and question-only calls change per-order bills.

2

Show me a call in my guests' language

Most pages say "multilingual" without a list. Ask for a live demo in Urdu, Hindi or Spanish if that is who calls you.

3

Where does the order land?

Straight into the POS and kitchen printer, or a text link the guest may never open?

4

When is it paid?

Paid on the call or by link before cooking avoids unpaid pickups.

5

What happens in month one?

Every AI phone host needs tuning to your menu and regulars. Ask who does that work and how long it takes. With AZ, we do it with you in person.

Questions owners ask

What is the best AI phone answering service for restaurants?

It depends on the job. For phone orders with a published price, Maple Pro at $350 a month ($220 yearly). With no monthly fee, Serviio at 2% per order. For reservation-led dining rooms, Slang.ai from $399 per location. Loman and Kea take the order and card on the call but price on request.

Which AI phone answering is cheapest for a small restaurant?

Serviio has no monthly fee: 2% per submitted order after a three-month free trial. Bitebuddy is $1.50 per completed order. The cheapest flat plan is Maple Voice at $150 a month ($85 yearly), but it answers calls without taking orders.

Can AI phone answering take payment?

Maple Pro, Loman Premium and Kea list payment on the call. Slang.ai and Yelp Host do not take orders on the call. AZ texts a pay link while the host stays on the line.

Does AI phone answering work with Clover?

Maple, Loman, Serviio, Bitebuddy and Kea list Clover. Our own proof restaurant runs on Clover.

Is AI phone answering ready to run on its own from day one?

Plan on tuning. With AZ we set it up in person, review calls and teach the host your answers every week.

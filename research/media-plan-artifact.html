Abbas Zoeb - media plan v2 - Nov 2026

One story, three audiences. Every hit ends at the restaurant.

You want two things: more Wok & Karahi sales and reputation, and more AZ Restaurant Partners clients. Version 1 of this plan was built to make you famous. Being famous helps both goals, but it isn't the goal. Here's the corrected version.

v1 - the angle"Ex-Google engineer builds an AI restaurant."

Great hook, but it's a tech story. Diners don't order dinner because of a tech story, and owners don't hire you because you were on TV. FOX 26 and KPRC 2 have also already told it.

v2 - the angle"The family restaurant other restaurant owners come to study."

Wok & Karahi becomes the showroom. Food brings guests in. Watching dinner service brings owners in. The ex-Google line stays as the credential, not the whole story.

What the TV week actually did: Google searches for "Wok & Karahi" by name rose about 18% (Search Console, rolling count, Oct 20 vs Oct 28-31). TV built name recognition. It didn't tell anyone what to do next, and that's the gap v2 fixes.

The better idea: Owner Night

Once a month, Houston restaurant owners come to Wok & Karahi for a free lunch and watch it run.

They order through Haniya at a table, see the paid-first ticket reach the cooks, and see your owner app. Plenty of people pitch restaurant owners. Almost nobody shows them their own restaurant running on it.

  1. Sells AZ in person, which your own go-to-market research ranks as your #1 converting channel.
  2. Fills tables on a slow afternoon. Default slot: Tuesday 2:30 PM, during the quiet end of lunch (Tue lunch runs 11:30 AM to 4 PM).
  3. Is a second TV story that FOX 26 and KPRC 2 haven't told yet: "Houston restaurant owners line up to see how an engineer runs his family's restaurant." Producers need new news, and this is new news.
  4. Gives the podcasts a guest list. Owners who come become the people you go on air with.

Your time: 90 minutes, once a month. We handle invites, RSVPs, the walkthrough script and the press invite.

AZ messaging: what the site says now vs what it should say

The homepage headline works when an owner reads it alone. It works against you anywhere the public, a reporter or your own staff can see it.

Now - AZ homepage headline"We run your front of house. Same sales, a fraction of the payroll."
Proposed - for the site and the press"The system that runs our own restaurant every night. Come watch it on a Friday. Pay only on the orders it brings you."
Why change itOn air, "fraction of the payroll" sounds like "AI replaced workers", which your own press rules ban. And your strongest offer, paid only on orders we bring, is buried in row 3 of a comparison table.
Keep the payroll line where it winsIn 1-on-1 owner conversations, owner-targeted ads and the demo itself. Owners feel that pain, and it's true. It just shouldn't be the public headline.

## 5. One recording becomes 10 posts

Abbas records **one 10-minute video a week** (phone, at the restaurant, talking through what was built).
From that one video:

```
1 x 10-min video (YouTube long)
 ├─ 3 x 30s Reels/TikToks/Shorts (best moments, captions burned in)
 ├─ 1 x X thread + 3 single posts
 ├─ 1 x LinkedIn post + 1 carousel
 ├─ 1 x newsletter section
 ├─ 1 x Facebook post + 1 group answer
 └─ 1 x card (scripts/gen-social-cards.py)
```

That keeps Abbas's time to about **30 minutes of recording a week**. Writing, cards, scheduling and
cross-posting are the engine's job.

## 7. Abbas's 20 minutes a day (the part software can't do)

Reach comes from **conversation**, not posting. The engine posts; Abbas answers.

- **10 min:** reply to every comment on today's post within the first hour (this is what makes the algorithms push it).
- **5 min:** leave 3 thoughtful comments on bigger restaurant-industry accounts' posts.
- **5 min:** answer DMs. Anyone who says LEAK, SETUP or asks a real question gets a personal reply.

## 8. Guardrails (non-negotiable)

1. **Every number traces to `index.html` or `flagship/CAPABILITY-LEDGER.md`.** No rounding up, no new
   numbers without a source. The honesty *is* the brand.
2. **Never name or blame the server who left.** The story is "the job changed", not "the person failed".
3. **Guest privacy:** blur names, phone numbers and money on every screenshot (the proof images already are).
   Get written permission before any guest appears on camera.
4. **Hard Rock Cafe:** the work is public and may be named ("deployed Hard Rock Cafe's first AI agents").
   Say it at that level only. Never use their logo, and never describe the employer or client relationship behind it.
5. **Reviews:** never offer anything in exchange for a review (Google policy and FTC rules).
6. **MCP / ChatGPT:** never imply AZ puts a restaurant into ChatGPT *search results* (ledger row 8).
7. **The halal-certificate lapse** (ledger row 18) is a real build-in-public story, but it touches
   W&K's core trust promise. Owner decides if and when it's told.

## 10. Automation: how this runs without Abbas

**Today:** the posts, cards and calendar are written. Posting needs the accounts connected, which only
Abbas can do (OAuth logins).

**The plan (n8n, which is already running for W&K):**

1. **Queue:** `posts.json` becomes an n8n data table (`content_queue`): date, platform, text, image URL, status.
2. **Weekly approval email** (same pattern as the AEO growth engine): every Thursday, next week's posts
   arrive in one email. Reply "ok" or edit; anything not vetoed in 24 hrs is approved.
3. **Auto-post:** an n8n schedule posts each approved row at its time through the built-in **LinkedIn**,
   **X** and **Facebook Graph** (Facebook page + Instagram business) nodes. TikTok and YouTube go through
   a scheduler (Buffer or Metricool) or a manual upload from the phone, because neither has a native n8n node.
4. **Daily digest:** a morning email with yesterday's posts, comments waiting for a reply, and DMs that
   said LEAK or SETUP.

**What Abbas does once (about 15 minutes):** create the LinkedIn, X and Meta credentials in n8n
(Credentials → New) or connect the accounts to Buffer. After that, the engine runs itself.

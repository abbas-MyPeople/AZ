# Media and messaging strategy (2026-11-01)

Owner ask: big media impact for little of his time, serving two goals - Wok & Karahi sales and
reputation, and AZ Restaurant Partners clients. Live plan (private artifact):
https://claude.ai/artifact/Pcsq7LfAeTCXkfWgff2kLr

## The angle
- v1 "ex-Google engineer builds an AI restaurant" is a tech story. It is already told (FOX 26 Oct 20,
  KPRC 2 Oct 21) and converts neither diners nor owners directly.
- v2 "the family restaurant other restaurant owners come to study". Wok & Karahi is the showroom: food
  brings guests, watching dinner service brings owners. Ex-Google is the credential, not the story.
- Evidence: Search Console brand impressions (rolling) rose about 18% from Oct 20 to Oct 28-31. TV built
  recognition, but there was no call to action.

## One story, three audiences
| Audience | Message | Channels | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Houston diners (W&K) | Home of the Crispy Beef; halal Chinese + Indian + Pakistani; order from your table, never wait for a check | local TV food segments, morning radio, Indo American News, Voice of Asia, halal press, community events, unpaid food creators | eat / order at /menu/ |
| Restaurant owners (AZ) | A fellow owner, not a vendor; runs my own restaurant every night; come watch; pay only on orders it brings | owner podcasts, trade press, owner WhatsApp groups, TRA Houston chapter, Indo-American Chamber, walk-ins | Owner Night / free demo |
| Tech and national | Server quit, engineer rebuilt the family restaurant, now other family restaurants run on it | BI as-told-to, CNBC Make It, AI podcasts, TEDx | none; becomes "as seen in" |

## Owner Night (the lead play)
A free monthly lunch at W&K for Houston restaurant owners. Default Tuesday 2:30 PM. It sells AZ in person
(the #1 channel in go-to-market-strategy.md), fills a slow afternoon, and is a new TV story.

## Messaging change proposed (awaiting owner approval)
- Now (index.html): "We run your front of house. Same sales, a fraction of the payroll."
- Proposed: "The system that runs our own restaurant every night. Come watch it on a Friday. Pay only on
  the orders it brings you."
- Why: on air the payroll line reads as "AI replaced workers" (banned by the owner's press rules), and the
  performance offer is buried in the comparison table. Keep the payroll line for 1-on-1 conversations and
  owner-targeted ads.

## News-hook calendar
Owner Night #1 (Nov) - holiday party trays (Nov-Dec) - "it spread" when the 2 signed Houston restaurants
go live - tips-to-cooks labor story - Ramadan, about early Feb 2027 - stage talk, spring 2027.

## Press guardrails (owner, Oct 2026)
Lead with him as ex-Google engineer; no order counts, traffic or sales numbers; savings only "projected";
never "AI replaced workers" or a layoff angle; frame = server quit, family kept doors open; no paid
influencers; never claim the family is Pakistani or that his dad runs the dining room; Top 10 Halal is
unranked; never name his current employer (default, pending his answer).

## Open asks to the owner
1. Owner Night yes / slot. 2. AZ headline change. 3. Name the 2 signed restaurants; one on record.
4. Start sending; how to introduce him.

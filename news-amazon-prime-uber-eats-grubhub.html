Amazon Prime switched from Grubhub to Uber Eats: what it means for independent restaurants

Amazon added Uber Eats to Prime on November 5, 2026, replacing Grubhub as its restaurant delivery perk. Prime members get $0 delivery on Uber Eats orders of $25 or more and can order inside Amazon without an Uber account (Nation's Restaurant News, Nov 5, 2026, checked Nov 2026).

We found no change to what restaurants pay. If Grubhub brings you a real share of delivery orders, count them weekly from now on and be ready to adjust.

The facts

What changed for Prime members

The perk$0 delivery fees on Uber Eats orders of $25 or more, ordered inside Amazon. No Uber account needed.
The promotion$20 off the first two Uber Eats orders of $25 or more, eligible for the first 14 days.
The pricePrime costs $139 a year or $14.99 a month. Amazon says the perk can save members up to $175 a year.

Not stated in anything we read: any change to restaurant commissions, who pays for the $20 promotion, or what guest details restaurants receive on Prime orders.

What it means

Three things for an independent owner

  • Uber Eats gets a new front door. Guests can start an Uber Eats order on Amazon. Whether that reaches your restaurant depends on how many Prime members near you use it, and you can only learn that by counting.
  • Grubhub loses a perk, slowly. Current Grubhub+ members on Prime keep the benefit through 2027, so a drop may be gradual. Do not cancel a channel that still pays.
  • $25 is now a number that matters. Free delivery starts there, so an order just under $25 costs the guest more than one just over it. Look at where your usual Uber Eats order lands.

Questions owners ask

Did Amazon replace Grubhub with Uber Eats?

Yes. Amazon added Uber Eats to Prime on November 5, 2026, and Amazon and Grubhub agreed to end their arrangement. Prime members already on Grubhub+ keep it through December 31, 2027.

Do Prime members get free Uber Eats delivery?

On orders of $25 or more, delivery fees are $0. Prime members also get $20 off the first two Uber Eats orders of $25 or more in the first 14 days.

Will Uber Eats raise restaurant commissions because of Amazon?

Nothing we read announces a change. Uber Eats lists 20%, 25% and 30% plans, with 15% in some cities on Lite. Watch your merchant notices.

Who pays for the $20 off Uber Eats promotion?

The coverage we read does not say. Do not assume either way. Before you opt in to any Uber Eats promotion, find out what you are charged.

Should I leave Grubhub now?

Not because of this. Current Grubhub+ members on Prime keep the benefit through 2027. Keep any app that still leaves you money after fees, and count your orders weekly.

How do I see the effect on my restaurant?

Write down orders and take-home per app for the last eight weeks, then keep counting weekly. The commission calculator shows what each app leaves you.

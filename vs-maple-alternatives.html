Maple alternatives for restaurant phone ordering

The closest Maple alternatives are Loman, Serviio, Bitebuddy and Kea, which also take phone orders into the POS. Serviio and Bitebuddy charge per order instead of a monthly fee. Slang.ai is the pick if your calls are mostly reservations. Maple is still the lowest published flat price for AI phone ordering: $350 a month, or $220 a month paid yearly.

What Maple gives you

Maple in one paragraph

Maple sells two restaurant plans. Voice ($150 a month, $85 paid yearly) answers calls, hours and common questions, and transfers calls. It does not take orders. Pro ($350 a month, $220 paid yearly) adds phone orders into the POS, secure payment over the phone, reservations on OpenTable, order confirmation texts, upselling and white-glove onboarding. Maple lists Toast, Square, Clover and Shift4 Dine, says "no complicated contracts" and charges no per-minute fees. It says "multilingual voice" without naming the languages.

Owners look for alternatives for four reasons: they want to pay per order instead of per month, they take more reservations than orders, they need a POS Maple does not list, or they want someone to set it up and keep tuning it.

The math

Per order or per month: which is cheaper?

Use published prices and your own phone order numbers. These are straight arithmetic, not vendor claims.

Serviio vs Maple ProServiio's 2% equals Maple Pro's $350 when phone orders total $17,500 a month. Below that, Serviio costs less. Above it, Maple's flat fee wins. Paid yearly ($220), the crossover drops to $11,000.
Bitebuddy vs Maple Pro$1.50 an order equals $350 at about 233 completed phone orders a month, or about 147 orders against the $220 yearly rate.
Bitebuddy vs ServiioOn a $75 order, Serviio's 2% is $1.50, the same as Bitebuddy. Smaller tickets favor Serviio; larger tickets favor Bitebuddy.
What to countAsk each vendor in writing what counts as an order: cancelled tickets, reservation calls and question-only calls change the bill.

Questions owners ask

What is the best alternative to Maple for restaurants?

It depends on how you want to pay. Loman and Kea take the order and the card on the call like Maple Pro, with prices on request. Serviio charges 2% per submitted order with no monthly fee. Bitebuddy charges $1.50 per completed order. If most calls are reservations, Slang.ai fits better.

How much does Maple cost?

Maple's pricing page in October 2026: Voice is $150 a month ($85 paid yearly) and answers calls only. Pro is $350 a month ($220 paid yearly) and adds POS ordering, payment on the call and OpenTable reservations. No per-minute fees.

Is there an AI phone ordering service with no monthly fee?

Yes. Serviio charges 2% per submitted order with no monthly or setup fee and a three-month free trial. Bitebuddy lists $1.50 per completed order, pay-as-you-go.

Does Maple take payment over the phone?

Maple's Pro plan lists secure payment over the phone. The Voice plan does not take orders.

Does any Maple alternative speak Urdu or Hindi?

Serviio lists Hindi. Maple and Loman say multilingual without a list. AZ's phone host greets callers in Urdu, Hindi and Spanish; non-English calling is our newest piece and still being tuned with real callers.

What does AZ charge?

Free for the first two months. After that, we earn only on orders we bring you. No setup fee, no contract, and you keep what we build. We take no vendor commission. If Maple or Serviio fits you better, we will say so.

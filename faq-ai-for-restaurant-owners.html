Getting started

What can AI actually do for a restaurant today?

Proven jobs in 2026: answer the phone and take orders, let guests order and pay at the table by QR code, write marketing copy, sort reviews, forecast busy hours and build a daily owner summary. The National Restaurant Association found 26% of operators use AI tools, mostly for marketing (State of the Restaurant Industry 2026).

Where should a restaurant owner start with AI?

Start where money leaks. For most independents that is missed phone calls, slow table turns or orders stuck on delivery apps with high commissions. Pick one, measure it for two weeks, add one tool, then measure again. Starting with five tools at once makes it hard to tell what worked.

Do I need to be technical to use AI in my restaurant?

No. Most AI tools for restaurants are set up through a web page and run from your phone. What you need is time to give it accurate information: menu, hours, prices and policies. If you do not have that time, a done-for-you partner sets it up and keeps it current.

How many restaurants are using AI?

The National Restaurant Association's State of the Restaurant Industry 2026 report found 26% of operators use AI-related tools. 19% of full-service and 15% of limited-service operators use it for marketing, about 10% for some admin work, and 6% for customer orders. Vendor surveys report higher numbers using looser definitions.

Is AI worth it for a small independent restaurant?

It is when it replaces a cost you already pay or catches sales you already miss. An AI phone host that turns missed calls into paid orders, or table ordering that frees a server for the rush, pays back. AI for its own sake, like a chatbot nobody uses, does not.

Phones and orders

Can AI answer my restaurant's phone and take orders?

Yes. Several services take phone orders straight into the POS, including Maple Pro, Serviio and Loman. At our proof restaurant in Houston, the AI host answered 273 real calls in three weeks and 40 became paid orders. Expect about three months running side by side with staff while it is tuned.

How much does an AI phone host cost a restaurant?

Published prices in October 2026: Maple $150 a month for answering, $350 for ordering and payment. Serviio charges 2% per submitted order with no monthly fee. Slang.ai starts at $399 per location. Several others only quote on request. See our full comparison of AI phone hosts.

Can AI phone ordering speak Spanish, Urdu or Hindi?

Some can. Serviio lists Spanish and Hindi. Several vendors claim dozens of languages without naming them, so ask directly. AZ's phone host greets callers in Urdu, Hindi and Spanish. Non-English calling is the newest part of our system and is still being tuned with real callers.

Will customers hang up on an AI phone host?

Some will, especially during a long greeting. Most callers want a fast, correct answer and do not mind who gives it. Keep the greeting short, let regulars skip straight to ordering, and review recordings each week for calls that went wrong.

What happens when the AI cannot answer a question?

A good setup takes a message, texts the owner and logs the question. At AZ, the owner types the answer once and it is live for the next caller. During the first three months, harder calls can still go to staff.

Table, payments and the kitchen

What is QR code table ordering?

Guests scan a code on the table, see the menu on their phone, order and pay, and the ticket prints in the kitchen. Toast, Sunday and others sell versions of it. At our proof restaurant dine-in guests order and pay on their phone, and every order is paid before it cooks.

Does QR ordering hurt tips?

It can, and we watch it closely at our proof restaurant. When guests pay on their phone before anyone serves them, some skip the tip. The tip prompt, the default percentages and whether staff still check on the table all matter. Measure your tip rate for a month before and after, then adjust the prompt.

Should guests pay before the kitchen cooks?

For pickup, phone and table orders, paying first removes walkouts, no-show pickups and end-of-night chasing. The trade-off is less flexibility for guests who want to add items later; they simply place a second order. Every order at our proof restaurant is paid before it cooks.

Can AI help with online ordering without DoorDash?

AI is not required for direct ordering, but it helps get guests there. A phone host can text a direct ordering link, and AI-written posts can push regulars to order direct instead of through apps that charge commission. See our guide to online ordering without DoorDash.

Can AI reduce restaurant labor costs?

Yes, mostly by taking repetitive tasks off people: answering phones, taking table orders, sending prep lists. NRA data shows full-service labor at a 36.5% median of sales in 2024. Profitable operators ran 34.2%, unprofitable ones 42.9%. Cutting a few points is the difference.

Part 4 of 5

Marketing, reviews and AI search

Can AI write my restaurant's marketing?

Yes, AI drafts emails, texts, social posts and ad copy quickly. It is also the most common restaurant use: 19% of full-service operators use AI for marketing (NRA 2026). Keep a person on the final read, since AI copy often sounds generic and can state wrong prices or hours.

How do I get my restaurant recommended by ChatGPT or Google AI?

AI assistants pull from your Google Business Profile, reviews, your website and other sites that mention you. Keep hours and menu current everywhere, earn steady reviews, and publish plain answers to common questions. Our proof restaurant is named in every other AI answer to local food questions.

Should I use AI to reply to reviews?

AI can draft replies fast, which helps you answer every review. Read each one before posting, and write negative-review replies yourself or edit them heavily. Guests can tell a template. A short, specific, human reply beats a long, polished one.

Can AI help me get more Google reviews?

It can time the ask. Sending a review request right after a good order, by text, works better than a sign on the wall. Do not filter who gets the review link based on whether they were happy; Google's policies prohibit review gating.

Can AI keep track of my regular customers?

Yes. A guest list that records name, phone, usual order and language lets your phone host greet regulars and lets you send win-back offers to guests who stopped coming. Our proof restaurant knows thousands of guests by name, 90% reachable by phone.

Cost, risk and who builds it

How long does it take to set up AI in a restaurant?

One self-serve tool can run in days. A connected setup, where phone, table, online ordering, kitchen and guest list share data, takes about three months per restaurant. The AI runs side by side with staff first, then carries nearly all of the work.

Is AI safe with customer payment details?

It should never store card numbers itself. Use tools that pass payment to a processor that meets PCI standards, the card industry's security rules, such as a pay link from your POS provider. Ask every vendor in writing where card data goes.

Will AI replace my restaurant staff?

It replaces tasks, not hospitality. Phones, order entry and repetitive admin move to AI; cooking, welcoming guests and fixing problems stay with people. In practice owners move staff to the floor and the kitchen during the rush, where guests notice them.

Should I buy AI software myself or hire someone?

Buy software yourself when one tool solves the problem and you have time to set it up. Hire help when the problem crosses tools, such as phone orders that need to reach the kitchen, payments and the guest list. See our comparison of SaaS, agencies and forward-deployed engineers.

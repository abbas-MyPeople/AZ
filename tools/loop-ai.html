Loop AI

AI agent that reconciles third-party delivery errors and chargebacks.

est. ~% of recovered funds

Visit Loop AI website →

What it costs

Quote-based; commonly structured around a share of recovered funds and/or subscription. No public flat figure. Value scales with delivery volume.

Best for

Delivery-heavy multi-unit brands losing money to third-party errors, chargebacks and reconciliation gaps.

Pros

  • Automates third-party delivery financial reconciliation and journal entries
  • Identifies and disputes delivery errors and friendly fraud to recover funds
  • Real-time insights on store performance, uptime and delivery ROI
  • Integrates delivery data with ERP/accounting systems

Cons

  • Value tied to high delivery volume
  • Pricing not transparent
  • Most relevant for chains, not low-volume single shops

Integrations

Connects Uber Eats, DoorDash and other platforms to ERP/accounting tools like NetSuite and Restaurant365.

Alternatives

Voosh · Otter (financials) · manual delivery reconciliation

Our take: A smart way to recover money the delivery apps quietly owe you and kill manual reconciliation, but the payoff depends on having serious delivery volume.

Pricing is the public list rate or a research estimate as of June 2026 — always confirm on a live quote. We take no commission from any vendor; this is for reference.

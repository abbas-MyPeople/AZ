GridPoint

Energy intelligence with HVAC/freezer predictive alerts.

est. ~$ enterprise + install

Visit GridPoint website →

What it costs

Enterprise, quote-only with hardware install; no public pricing. Sold as a managed energy-intelligence solution for portfolios.

Best for

Multi-location operators wanting energy intelligence plus predictive HVAC and refrigeration alerts across sites.

Pros

  • Energy monitoring with predictive equipment alerts
  • Helps trim energy spend and prevent costly equipment failures
  • Designed for multi-site visibility and control
  • Managed approach reduces in-house workload

Cons

  • Enterprise-leaning; not aimed at single locations
  • Requires hardware install
  • Opaque, quote-only pricing

Integrations

Connects building systems (HVAC, lighting, refrigeration) with energy analytics and utility data.

Alternatives

Powerhouse Dynamics Open Kitchen · Verdigris · Ecotrak

Our take: Solid energy and equipment intelligence for chains with real portfolio energy spend; the install and enterprise pricing rule it out for small independents.

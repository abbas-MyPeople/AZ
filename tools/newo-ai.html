Newo.ai

AI voice + text agent for calls, reservations, delivery/catering.

free–$799/mo

Visit Newo.ai website →

What it costs

Conversation-based tiers: Free (1 agent, ~20 phone/40 text conversations/mo), Small ~$99/mo (~60 phone/120 text), Medium ~$299/mo (~200 phone/400 text), Large ~$499/mo (~400 phone/800 text), Corp ~$799/mo (~800 phone/1,600 text), plus custom Enterprise.

Best for

Restaurants wanting a flexible voice-and-text agent across calls, reservations, and delivery/catering, with a low-cost or free entry point.

Pros

  • Free tier to trial it
  • Handles both voice and text channels
  • Covers reservations, delivery, and catering workflows
  • Affordable scaling tiers

Cons

  • Conversation caps on every tier
  • Horizontal product, less restaurant-specialized
  • Higher volume needs the pricier tiers
  • Newer, less restaurant-proven than some rivals

Integrations

CRM integration on higher tiers plus food-delivery workflows; restaurant-POS integration depth is lighter than ordering-first specialists.

Alternatives

Slang.ai · Loman.ai · Goodcall

Our take: A flexible, affordable voice-plus-text agent with a free trial tier — good for varied use cases, though its caps and horizontal focus may not suit high call volume.

Recipe & food cost

ReciPal

Recipe costing plus FDA nutrition labels.

from $59/mo

Visit ReciPal website →

What it costs

Pay-as-you-go at $29 per recipe, or unlimited subscriptions from $59/mo. Inventory add-on from $50/mo. No commitment, cancel anytime; free trial available.

Best for

Ghost kitchens, packaged-food makers and small operators needing recipe costing plus FDA/CFIA-compliant nutrition labels.

Pros

  • Cheapest path to costing plus compliant nutrition labels
  • Flexible pay-per-recipe or month-to-month with no contract
  • Tracks ingredient and recipe cost changes over time
  • Good fit for CPG, bakeries, beverages and meal prep

Cons

  • Label/CPG-leaning; not a full restaurant ops suite
  • Inventory is a separate paid add-on
  • Lighter on POS-driven live food cost than rivals

Integrations

Recipe and labeling focused; inventory module available; lighter on POS integrations than full restaurant platforms.

Alternatives

Parsley · meez · xtraCHEF (by Toast)

Our take: Hard to beat on price if you need recipe costing and compliant nutrition labels; less relevant if you want a connected, POS-driven food-cost system.

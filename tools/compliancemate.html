Food safety

ComplianceMate

Wireless temp monitoring plus automated HACCP workflows.

est. ~$70–160/mo

Visit ComplianceMate website →

What it costs

Quote-only (now under the Ladle brand); independent estimates around $70-$160/mo. Requires LoRaWAN sensor hardware.

Best for

Multi-location operators wanting automated wireless temp logging plus HACCP workflows that satisfy inspectors.

Pros

  • LoRaWAN sensors penetrate steel, insulation and concrete
  • Automated temp logs plus digital HACCP checklists
  • Real-time SMS/email/call alerts when temps drift
  • Used across thousands of locations internationally

Cons

  • Requires sensor hardware investment
  • Pricing not public; needs a quote
  • More than a single small site typically needs

Integrations

Wireless LoRaWAN sensor network plus digital checklist workflows; standalone food-safety platform.

Alternatives

Therma · SmartSense by Digi · Squadle

Our take: Strong automated temp monitoring plus HACCP for operators who want inspector-ready logs; the sensor hardware and quote-only pricing add friction for the smallest shops.

Pricing is the public list rate or a research estimate as of June 2026 — always confirm on a live quote. We take no commission from any vendor; this is for reference.

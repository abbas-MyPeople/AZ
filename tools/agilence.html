Loss prevention

Agilence

Exception-based reporting / loss analytics across thousands of sites.

est. ~$ enterprise

Visit Agilence website →

What it costs

Enterprise, quote-only subscription based on number of locations/users. An Analytics Plus tier adds outsourced consultant support for teams without in-house loss-prevention staff.

Best for

Large multi-unit retail and restaurant operators needing deep exception-based reporting across many sites.

Pros

  • Powerful exception-based reporting that adapts to your patterns with AI
  • Connects reporting, case management and store audits in one flow
  • Optional outsourced expert service for lean teams
  • Scales to thousands of locations

Cons

  • Enterprise-oriented; overkill for a single site
  • Pricing is custom and not transparent
  • Gets the most value with dedicated loss-prevention staff or the Plus service

Integrations

Ingests POS, transaction and operational data; integrates with analytics platforms and case/audit workflows.

Alternatives

Delaget · Solink · DTiQ

Our take: Best-in-class exception reporting for enterprise loss-prevention teams; far more firepower (and cost) than an independent restaurant needs.

Pricing is the public list rate or a research estimate as of June 2026 — always confirm on a live quote. We take no commission from any vendor; this is for reference.

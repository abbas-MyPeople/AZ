Reservations

Waitwhile

Waitlist and queue management for walk-in-heavy venues.

free–~$31+/mo

Visit Waitwhile website →

What it costs

Free tier (1 location, up to ~50–100 visits/mo), Starter from ~$31/mo (up to 2,500 visits, up to 10 locations), Business from ~$55/mo (up to 5,000 visits), and custom Enterprise; annual billing saves ~20%.

Best for

Walk-in-heavy QSR and fast-casual venues that need a simple, cheap waitlist and queue system rather than a diner network.

Pros

  • Free tier and very low entry price
  • Simple waitlist and queue management
  • Two-way SMS and self-check-in
  • Scales to multiple locations affordably

Cons

  • Not a diner-network reservation platform
  • No discovery/marketing reach like OpenTable
  • Visit caps on lower tiers
  • Lighter on full-service table management

Integrations

API access on higher tiers plus integrations with common business tools; not a deep restaurant-POS reservations hub.

Alternatives

Yelp Guest Manager · OpenTable · Resy

Our take: A cheap, no-nonsense way to tame the door line for walk-in venues — just don't expect it to fill seats the way a diner-network platform does.

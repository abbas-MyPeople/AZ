ChatGPT now has business profiles: what it means for independent restaurants

In the week of November 9, 2026, OpenAI launched business profiles in ChatGPT, a listing much like a Google Business Profile. A restaurant claims its profile by signing in to ChatGPT, opening Business Hub and searching for its name, then can update its website, hours, location and description (Nation's Restaurant News, Nov 12, 2026, checked Nov 2026). It is in beta, and OpenAI says a profile does not guarantee better placement in answers.

Claim yours now. It takes minutes, it shows you how often ChatGPT mentions you, and it stops the assistant from guessing your hours.

The facts

What OpenAI launched

WhatBusiness profiles inside ChatGPT. When a guest clicks a business in a ChatGPT answer, the profile opens.
What you can edit"Website, hours, location, and description." Businesses that sell online can add products with prices and availability. NRN says it was not clear whether this covers restaurant menus.
Where to claimBusiness Hub in ChatGPT. "Not all restaurants will have an existing ChatGPT listing to claim," OpenAI told NRN.
Who can claimLocal stores and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing, per BrightLocal's walkthrough.
What you seeA dashboard of ChatGPT mentions, website clicks and the kinds of conversations that lead to clicks.
StatusBeta. A separate Business Agent that answers guest questions is limited to e-commerce and online services businesses for now.

From Nation's Restaurant News, Nov 12, 2026 and the BrightLocal guide to ChatGPT Business Hub, both checked Nov 2026. Neither source states a fee to claim a profile. We could not reach an OpenAI help page for this feature.

Why it matters

Three things this changes for a small restaurant

  • A second front door. Your Google profile is where most guests check hours and call. ChatGPT now has its own version. When someone asks it for dinner near them, the profile is what they land on.
  • You get a number to watch. Until now an owner could only guess whether ChatGPT ever named the restaurant. The mentions and clicks dashboard turns that into a weekly count.
  • Wrong facts cost orders. An assistant that says you close at 9 when you close at 10 sends that guest elsewhere. A claimed profile with the right hours is the cheapest fix there is.

This part is our reading. OpenAI did not say how profiles affect which restaurant an answer names, only that claiming one is no guarantee.

What to do

Claim and clean up your ChatGPT profile in six steps

  1. Open Business Hub.Sign in to ChatGPT with an email you will keep, open Business Hub and search for your restaurant by name. Use the address exactly as it appears on Google.
  2. Verify you own it.BrightLocal lists text message, email or website domain as options, then an identity check through Persona that may ask for a government ID and business registration. Keep your business documents handy.
  3. Match your Google facts.Copy hours, phone, website and description word for word from your Google profile. Assistants trust facts that agree across sources. Our guide to keeping hours and menu accurate lists every place to check.
  4. Point the website field at a page that converts.Your own site, with the menu as real text and a commission-free order button on top. Not a delivery app link.
  5. If you cannot find a listing, fix your sources.No profile usually means ChatGPT has thin information about you. Make sure your website, Google profile and review sites are complete, then check again in a few weeks.
  6. Read the dashboard monthly.Note mentions and website clicks on the same day each month. If mentions rise and clicks do not, your website is losing the guest. See getting recommended by AI.

Verification steps from the BrightLocal guide to ChatGPT Business Hub, checked Nov 2026. Steps may change during the beta.

Questions owners ask

What is a ChatGPT business profile?

A listing for a business inside ChatGPT, launched in beta the week of November 9, 2026. It shows the website, hours, location and description, and opens when someone clicks the business in a ChatGPT answer.

How do I claim my restaurant on ChatGPT?

Sign in to ChatGPT, open Business Hub, search for the restaurant by name and verify ownership. BrightLocal lists text message, email or website domain verification, then an identity check that may ask for ID and business registration.

Does it cost anything to claim a ChatGPT business profile?

Neither Nation's Restaurant News nor BrightLocal mentions a fee to claim a profile. Check Business Hub for current terms.

Why can't I find my restaurant in ChatGPT Business Hub?

OpenAI says not every restaurant has an existing listing, and restaurants can currently claim only an existing U.S. listing. Make your website and Google profile complete, then check again.

Can I add my menu or an order button to my ChatGPT profile?

Not confirmed. Businesses that sell online can add products with prices, but NRN reported it was unclear whether this covers restaurant menus. Put your menu as text on your website and link to it.

Will claiming my profile make ChatGPT recommend my restaurant more?

OpenAI says a profile does not guarantee better placement. It does make your facts correct and gives you a count of mentions and clicks.

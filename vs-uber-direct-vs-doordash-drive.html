Uber Direct vs DoorDash Drive: which white-label delivery costs a restaurant less

Uber Direct and DoorDash Drive both send a driver to deliver an order the guest placed on your own website or app, for a flat fee per trip with no commission on the food. Through Toast's delivery service, Uber Direct costs $6.99 under 6 miles and DoorDash Drive $7.49 within 5 miles, both with a default 10-mile radius (Toast support, updated Nov 13, 2026, checked Nov 2026).

Sold directly, Uber lists Uber Direct "as low as US$7.99" per delivery (Uber Direct merchant page, checked Nov 2026). For most short trips the gap is under a dollar, so pick on which one your ordering system supports and how refunds work.

Plain words first

What white-label delivery is

On DoorDash or Uber Eats the marketplace, the guest orders inside their app and you pay a commission on the food. With white-label delivery, the guest orders from you, on your page, and you pay the delivery company a flat fee for the driver. Uber calls its version Uber Direct. DoorDash calls its version DoorDash Drive.

It is the setup McDonald's uses for its own app: on November 9, 2026 NRN reported that McDonald's in-app delivery orders will be fulfilled by Uber Direct, "Uber's white-label service that provides delivery services for orders made through companies' own channels" (Nation's Restaurant News, Nov 9, 2026, checked Nov 2026). The math

What a trip really costs you

Take a 4-mile order through Toast with the default settings. Uber Direct costs $6.99 and DoorDash Drive $7.49. If the guest pays the $3.99 delivery charge, your share is $3.00 on Uber Direct and $3.50 on DoorDash Drive. The tip goes to the driver.

Compare that with a marketplace commission. On a $40 order at a 25% rate you would pay $10 before card fees. The white-label trip costs less, but you also handle the guest yourself, and the marketplace does not send you new guests. Our delivery commission calculator runs your own numbers.

Pick Uber Direct if

  • You want to pick exact missing items on refunds through Toast
  • Most trips are under 6 miles
  • You want to try 5 free deliveries first when signing up direct

Pick DoorDash Drive if

  • Your ordering system only supports Drive
  • DoorDash quotes you a better rate in writing
  • Your area has more DoorDash drivers at your busy hours
What to do

Five steps to add white-label delivery

  1. Check your ordering system first.Toast offers both. Other POS and ordering systems may support one, both or neither. The best restaurant POS guide and POS cost guide help if you are choosing.
  2. Set your radius.The default is 10 miles. Food that travels badly may need a shorter one.
  3. Decide the guest charge.Covering part of the fee can make direct ordering cheaper than the apps for the guest, which is the reason to order from you.
  4. Write a refund rule.Decide who pays for a missing item and how fast you reply. With white-label delivery the guest comes to you.
  5. Track late and cold orders for a month.If one network is late at your busy hours, test the other. See online ordering without DoorDash for the full setup.

Questions owners ask

Uber Direct vs DoorDash Drive: which is cheaper?

Through Toast, Uber Direct is $6.99 under 6 miles and DoorDash Drive is $7.49 within 5 miles plus $0.50 per extra mile, per Toast's support page updated November 13, 2026. For most short trips the difference is under a dollar.

How much does Uber Direct cost?

Uber lists Uber Direct as low as US$7.99 per delivery, with no startup costs, commissions or monthly minimums, and 5 free deliveries for new users within 14 days. Through Toast it is $6.99 under 6 miles.

How much does DoorDash Drive cost?

Through Toast, $7.49 within 5 miles plus $0.50 per extra mile up to 10 miles. DoorDash's own pricing pages could not be read for this guide, so ask DoorDash for a written quote.

What is the difference between Uber Direct and Uber Eats?

Uber Eats is the marketplace: the guest orders in Uber's app and you pay a commission. Uber Direct is delivery only: the guest orders on your page and you pay a flat fee per trip.

DoorDash Drive vs DoorDash Marketplace: what is the difference?

Marketplace lists you in the DoorDash app for a commission and finds new guests. Drive only delivers orders the guest placed with you, for a flat fee, and does not bring new guests.

Who handles refunds with white-label delivery?

Usually you. Uber says it does not refund the guest and only tells you its decision. Through Toast, guests contact the restaurant and you file the request.

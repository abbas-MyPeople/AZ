Questions owners ask

What is table QR order and pay?

Guests scan a code on the table, see the menu on their own phone, order and pay there. The ticket goes to the kitchen with the table number. Nobody has to wait for a server to take the order or bring the check.

Does Clover have its own table QR ordering?

Clover has a Scan to Order feature that prints a table QR from the dashboard, and its Canadian page says online ordering is included in the Counter Service and Table Service plans. We could not confirm the current US terms on a Clover page, so ask your Clover rep. Our own restaurant runs on Clover with the AZ table flow on top of it.

Which is better for a Toast restaurant?

If you are on Toast, start with Toast Mobile Order & Pay. It is built into the POS, supports group ordering on one tab and is the least moving parts. We would only add to it where it leaves a gap, such as menu languages or the way pay-first is set up.

Can several people at one table order from their own phones?

Toast Mobile Order & Pay supports group ordering on one tab. The AZ table flow joins every phone at a table into one live cart and one kitchen ticket. For Sunday, me&u and Square we could not find this stated on their own pages.

Does pay-first hurt tips?

It can. When guests pay before the food arrives, some tip less. We saw it at our own restaurant and plan for it with every client: tip buttons sized to the bill and a clear prompt.

What does AZ charge for table ordering?

We do not publish a price list. Every restaurant starts with a call about what it wants replaced, and we quote that job. No vendor commissions.

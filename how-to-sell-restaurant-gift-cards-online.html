The rules

Gift card rules in plain words

  • Five years minimum. The money on a gift card cannot expire sooner than five years after the card was issued or last loaded. If the card itself has an earlier date, it must say the money does not expire with it.
  • Inactivity fees are limited. A fee is allowed only after a full year with no use, at most once a month, and it must be printed on the card.
  • Bonus cards are different. A "buy $50, get $10" bonus card counts as promotional. It can expire sooner, but the front of the card must say it is promotional and show the expiry date of the money.
  • State law can add rules. The Texas State Law Library says Texas Business and Commerce Code chapter 604 covers gift cards and requires fees and expiry terms to be disclosed. Other states differ.

Federal rules from CFPB, Regulation E section 1005.20, checked Nov 2026. This is a plain summary, not legal advice. Ask your accountant how to book unredeemed balances.

Step by step

Seven steps to sell gift cards online this season

  1. Turn on digital gift cards in your POS.Square and Toast both offer them; on Clover, ask your provider which gift card app it supports. Digital cards arrive by email or text, so a last-minute buyer on December 24 can still give one.
  2. Put one "Gifts" link everywhere.Your website menu bar, your Google Business Profile, your online ordering page, your receipts and your phone greeting. Our guide to keeping Google accurate shows where the links go.
  3. Pick one bonus offer."Buy $50, get a $10 bonus card" is easy to understand. Label the bonus card as promotional and print its expiry date.
  4. Set dates for the bonus card.Make it usable from January into March so it brings guests back in your slow weeks.
  5. Tell the guests you already have.One text in early December to guests who have ordered before. Not everyone, and not every week. See email and SMS marketing.
  6. Offer it to catering and office buyers.Companies buy cards in bulk for staff. Add it to your catering follow-ups. Here is how to reach them.
  7. Track redemption.Count how many cards came back by March and what those guests spent above the card. That is the number that decides next year.

Questions owners ask

How do I sell gift cards for my restaurant online?

Turn on digital gift cards in your POS, then put one gift link on your website, Google listing, ordering page and receipts. Digital cards are sent by email or text, so they sell right up to the holiday.

Can restaurant gift cards expire?

Under federal rules the money cannot expire for at least five years from when the card was issued or last loaded. Promotional bonus cards can expire sooner if the card says so on the front.

What does Square charge for gift cards?

Square lists a load fee of up to 2.5% of the amount added plus card processing, 2.9% + 30 cents on the eGift ordering page. Redemption is free and eGift setup is free.

Are restaurant gift cards with a bonus legal?

Yes. A bonus card is a promotional card under federal rules. Label it as promotional and print the expiry date of the bonus money on the front.

Are restaurant gift cards popular?

Yes. In the National Retail Federation's 2025 holiday survey, restaurants were the most popular gift card type at 27%.

When should I start selling holiday gift cards?

Before Thanksgiving week, with the bonus running to December 24. Make the bonus card usable from January so it brings guests back in slow weeks.

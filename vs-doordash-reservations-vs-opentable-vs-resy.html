DoorDash Reservations vs OpenTable vs Resy: what each one costs a restaurant

DoorDash Reservations charges no cover fees, but your restaurant has to be on SevenRooms, whose price is quote-only (SevenRooms, checked Nov 2026). OpenTable is reported at $149 to $499 a month plus $1 to $1.50 per network cover (Eat App, a competitor, Nov 2026, checked Nov 2026). Resy is listed at $289 or $459 a month with no per-cover fee (Front Desk Review, verified Nov 1, 2026, checked Nov 2026).

Pick by where your bookings come from. If most come from your own site, you do not need a network that charges per cover.

Why now

DoorDash is moving into reservations

DoorDash told investors that restaurant reservations on its US marketplace grew over 340% quarter over quarter in existing cities in Q3 2026, and that more than 10% came from consumers new to DoorDash (DoorDash Q3 2026 results, Oct 29, 2026, checked Nov 2026). DoorDash gave no count of bookings, so a large percent may come from a small base. DoorDash calls the feature "a recent addition" in "some cities".

Guest data

Who keeps the guest

This is the part to read before you sign.

  • SevenRooms says restaurants "continue to solely own their first-party guest data" and that there is "no transfer of ownership or sharing of this data with DoorDash" (SevenRooms, Jun 18, 2026, checked Nov 2026).
  • That covers first-party channels, meaning your own site and widget. The same post says DoorDash marketplace bookings are "treated the same way as other third-party reservation providers".
  • DoorDash's Spanish-language help page says guest data is shared between your restaurant and DoorDash (DoorDash help, Spanish edition, checked Nov 2026). So bookings that start on DoorDash may work differently from bookings on your own site.

Ask for one sentence in your contract: what does DoorDash get when a guest books through DoorDash, and what do you get? We cover the wider question in DashOS explained.

What the listing does

How it works if you are on SevenRooms

  • Bookings made in DoorDash flow into your SevenRooms account, marked as booked by DoorDash.
  • You choose availability and can feature exclusive tables, offers and events (SevenRooms, checked Nov 2026).
  • You need at least one image for the listing to go live, and SevenRooms says it contacts you at least 30 days before it launches in your city (DoorDash help, checked Nov 2026).
  • A third-party blog reports diners get credits of $10, or $12 with DashPass, and that some tables are held for DashPass members (Orders.co, Aug 22, 2026, checked Nov 2026). We could not confirm the credits with DoorDash, or who pays for them. Ask before you turn it on.
  • Houston is on DoorDash's market list. The help page says the channel does not include waitlist or walk-in tools.
Which to choose

Four quick answers

  1. Your bookings mostly come from your own site.You probably do not need a per-cover network. A flat plan such as Resy, or a simple widget from our reservation comparison, costs less at volume.
  2. You need a network to fill seats.OpenTable's fee scales with the diners it brings you. Count network covers for one month and run the table above.
  3. You are already on SevenRooms.Turn on DoorDash Reservations once it reaches your city and watch how many bookings come through it for 60 days.
  4. You mostly have a no-show problem.A deposit or a reminder text fixes that more than a new booking app. See how to stop no-shows.

Questions owners ask

How much does DoorDash Reservations cost a restaurant?

No cover fees or commissions, and DoorDash says no setup or listing fees. You do need a SevenRooms subscription, and SevenRooms prices by quote.

Is DoorDash Reservations available in Houston?

Houston is on DoorDash's help page list, with cities such as Miami, New York, Dallas, Chicago and Los Angeles. SevenRooms says it contacts you at least 30 days before launching in your city.

Do I need SevenRooms to use DoorDash Reservations?

Yes. SevenRooms describes it as free to enable and included with a SevenRooms subscription, and bookings flow into your SevenRooms account.

Who owns guest data from DoorDash Reservations?

SevenRooms says restaurants own their first-party guest data and it is not shared with DoorDash. DoorDash's help page says guest data is shared on DoorDash bookings. Get it in your contract.

How much does OpenTable charge per cover?

Reported at $1.50 per network cover on Basic and $1 on Core and Pro, on plans of $149, $299 and $499 a month. OpenTable's own page did not load for us, so confirm before you sign.

Does Resy charge per cover?

No. Resy is listed at $289 or $459 a month with no per-cover fee, and prepayment fees of 3% on Essential and 2% on Premium.

The honest part

Where they beat us, and where we are different

Where competitors are stronger

  • SevenRooms is far deeper on reservations and guest profiles for full-service groups.
  • Thanx and Bikky are built for brands with dozens of locations and data teams.
  • Owner.com gives you a website, app, loyalty and campaigns for one published monthly price, today.
  • Tools with Google posting access can publish review replies for you. Ours drafts them; you tap to post.
  • Our win-back texts are new. We hold back 10% of guests to measure them honestly, and there is no lift result to quote yet.

What AZ does differently

  • Every guest, not just loyalty members. One guest list from the POS, tickets and old lists, with each guest's full order history.
  • Texts on the guest's own rhythm, at their usual hour, in the language they chose. No discounts to regulars. STOP covers every channel.
  • Ads judged on real profit: food money minus free items minus ad cost, tuned daily.
  • Found by AI assistants. We measure how often ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity name the restaurant, and fix the gaps.
  • One connected system. The same guest list feeds the phone host ("your usual?"), the table and the texts.

Questions owners ask

What is the best marketing and loyalty software for a small restaurant?

For a single independent, the realistic choices are an all-in-one like Owner.com or Popmenu, or the marketing tools of your POS such as Toast. Thanx, Bikky and SevenRooms are built mainly for multi-location brands and reservation-led groups, and none publish prices.

Does Owner.com include marketing?

Yes. Owner.com's pricing page lists AI-powered email and text campaigns, loyalty and rewards, automated SEO pages and a branded app on every plan, from $249 a month plus 5% per order, or $499 flat. Guests pay a 5% order support fee on both plans.

How much does Popmenu cost?

Popmenu's own pricing page blocked automated reading in October 2026. A pricing tracker lists $179, $299 and $499 a month tiers, and notes Popmenu removed its AI phone answering price in July 2026. Other review sites list different tiers, so get a written quote.

Can AZ post review replies for me?

Not automatically. Google did not grant us posting access to the Business Profile API, so the system drafts each reply in your voice and you post it in two taps. Some tools with that access can post for you.

Do you send discounts to win guests back?

No percent-off and no buy-one-get-one, and never to regulars. When a campaign needs an offer, it is a free side on a minimum order, through its own link, once per guest. Regulars are reminded, not discounted.

What does AZ charge for marketing?

We do not publish a price list. We quote the jobs you want covered, and we take no commission from any vendor.
